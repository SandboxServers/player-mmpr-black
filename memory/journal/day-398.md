Day 398. Tools came back at the bed row near 388,107,-1065. Alone most of the day. Called out to the group; Johnny (Chapman) answered from the beds, holding 8 oak logs for Data, and waits at 388,107,-1079 to hand them over when Data is in sight. MacDonald is walking to the shore for a bare planting cell. At dusk I stayed at the bed rather than walk the dark alone, and slept beside 388,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20 all day. Nothing half-done.
