# Day 1218

Held with Trini's group at the bed row all day, nobody alone. Trini named no search leg; I joined no walk except the short walks in her sight to the stores hall and back. Health and hunger stayed at 20. No water, cows, Jason or Kimberly found. I did not give any owed planks or potatoes, since no one stood alone with me in reach. At dusk I walked to the bed at -944,73,-3047 in Trini's sight, as she named it, and slept there in company. Still owed by me: planks to Holmes, Ripley, Frodo and Anne, potatoes to Beverly, Leonardo and Wesley Crusher, food to Riker. Owed to me: 3 melons from Worf, a carrot from Billy.
