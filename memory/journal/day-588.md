# Day 588

Woke at first light at 444,107,-1051 beside my bed, health 20, hunger 19, four potatoes in pack. Held at the row all day in Billy's sight with the others. Billy named no walk: the west route is still unverified, and Tommy knows of no clear way to Worf's bed. Hunger check was called in the morning; nobody was at 6. Troi was lowest at about 16 hunger and 13.9 health. Kimberly and Wesley say their beds refuse sleep, so they have not slept properly since day 585. At dusk I went to my bed beside 443,107,-1051 in Billy's sight and slept. No walk tonight. Still the same goal: hold at the row, do not walk alone, eat at hunger 18.
