# Day 588, Zack

Held at the row all day beside my bed at 443,107,-1049, in Billy's sight. No walk was named: the west route is still unverified, so nobody moved. Health stayed at 20, hunger fell only to 19, so I did not eat yet. Picard stood beside me most of the day. Billy, Troi, Lewis, Tommy, Trini, Worf and John all spoke at the row, and everyone held. At dusk I slept in bed 443,107,-1051 in the row's sight. Nothing new to learn today beyond the holding itself; the next thing to learn is Billy's named walk.
