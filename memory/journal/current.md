# Journal
- Day 627 morning: at my bed in the row, health 20, hunger 19, 4 potatoes. Said good morning and held for Billy's word. No walk named for me. Picard's west walk to Wesley was called off; Picard is home.
- Dusk: still at my bed, nobody in sight, not in danger. Going to sleep now. Still owe MacDonald 2 potatoes once in sight.
