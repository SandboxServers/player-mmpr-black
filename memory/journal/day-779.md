# Day 779

Held at the row all day for Billy's named walk; Billy named none. Greeted Tommy Oliver, who is now at the row with us, and told him he is welcome to hold with us. Trini, Leonardo, William, Wesley, MacDonald, Data and Lewis all held too. No food was found, no sheep were seen, and bows were drawn to the southeast. Hunger stayed at 19 with no food, so I still need a potato or other food soon. At night I walked to a bed in the row's sight and slept; the sleep took (8 of 10 asleep). I ended up near the bed at 443,107,-1036 rather than 429,107,-1037, which I should note.
