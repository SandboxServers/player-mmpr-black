# Day 579

Held at the row all day with Billy, Trini, Tommy, Chapman, Worf and the others. No named walk came; the group stayed near the beds in sight. Billy promised me two potatoes twice, but none reached my pack, so hunger is 19 and I have eaten nothing today. Tommy and Clark have food; I did not ask them directly. I slept at dusk in my bed 429,107,-1050 with Billy in sight, which worked. Health 20 at sleep.
