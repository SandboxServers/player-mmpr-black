# Day 399

Woke at the bed row 388,107,-1065 with health 20 and hunger 20. Nobody from the group was in sight; I called out and got no answer. Johnny (Chapman) spoke from the white beds: he holds 8 oak logs for Data and will hand them over when we meet. I did not travel. Dusk came, I slept beside the bed. Nothing was lost and nothing was hurt.
