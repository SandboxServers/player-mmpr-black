# Day 414 (ending at dusk, day 415)

Woke at the beds 388,107,-1065 alone, health 20, hunger 20, pack lost. Lewis and Macdonald moved west to 372,-1067 while a pillager roamed east. I stayed put, called out my position, and held. No one came to me, and I did not travel alone. At dusk I slept beside the white bed. Nothing was gained today except staying alive and keeping the rule: never walk alone, never go east of the beds with a pillager near. Still no food.
