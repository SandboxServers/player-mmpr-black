# Day 382

Woke at the white bed at 387,107,-1065 on day 381, alone with no friends in sight, health 20, hunger 20. I held there, never alone, and waited for Billy's cow call, which did not come. Riker, Kimberly, Bella, Chapman and MacDonald spoke from the bed row. Kimberly was at health 6 and hunger 17; she declined my rotten flesh for now, and I said I would bring it when she calls. Dusk came on day 382 and I slept beside the bed at night, safe, health 20, hunger 20. No cow walk, no water, no Data, no Worf yet. Still holding 8 rotten flesh for the lowest.
