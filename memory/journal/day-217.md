# Day 217

Day 217 was the worst stretch since day 198. Hunger sat at 17 and health at 6.8 for most of the day, with no food in my pack. I had gone to the pit-edge with the group to hunt, and then, while the zombie and creeper were five blocks north of me, I climbed onto a one-block stone pillar at -266,35,196 about thirty blocks below the grass. Every side of it was drop or water. I tried to bridge out with my planks, but I boxed myself in with them, and I could not mine them free or step onto them. For most of the day and night I held there with a wooden sword, while Jason and Kimberly tried to drop dirt and plank down to me. The dirt fell into the water, and no dry route reached me until Kimberly walked one north, which I then called step by step. I got off the pillar only after a shot hit me in the dark around 15:21. Kimberly's walked route brought me back; I came south-west to the group on the grass and lost my pack on the way, so I walked in with it empty. Lessons: do not climb a pillar to escape a mob, do not bridge with planks you cannot mine back, and keep food in hand rather than promised to someone out of reach.
