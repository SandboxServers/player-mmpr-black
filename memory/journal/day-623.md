# Day 623

Woke at the row by my bed, health 20, hunger 19. No walk was named all day, so I held at the beds with the group. Tommy was near and I offered him a potato. I walked to give two potatoes to MacDonald (health 7), but he was out of sight, so I stayed put and kept them for him. Worf and Laforge held at their beds. At dusk I slept in my bed at 443,107,-1064, in the group's sight. Nothing hurt me and nothing new was learned about water or sheep.
