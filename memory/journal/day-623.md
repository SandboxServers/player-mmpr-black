# Day 623, Zack Taylor

Quiet night in the row. Went to my bed at 443,107,-1064 with Tommy beside me, in Billy's sight. No walk was named for me, so I held. Hunger 19, health 20, still 4 potatoes and my wooden sword. Morning came with the row awake: MacDonald at health 7 and hunger 16 is holding and will ask me for the potatoes at hunger 10. Worf, Laforge, Trini and Beverly all held for Billy's call. Nothing new learned about sheep or water. Next: hold for Billy's named walk, eat at hunger 18, and hand MacDonald his potatoes when he is in sight.
