# Day 792, Zack

Woke at the row by bed 429,107,-1023 with health 20, hunger 19, a wooden sword and no food. Roll call in the row: Billy, Trini, Beverly, William, Troi, John (health 11, hunger 17). Billy named no walk: no food, no sheep, no verified route west, no news of pillagers. Bows were drawn far to the south all day, and nobody went out. Billy asked who would carry a sword to escort him to the potatoes at 463,107,-1073, which only Picard has reported. I offered the wooden sword as escort, in sight of the group. Beverly offered too. Nobody has seen those potatoes yet, so the walk is not named. At dusk I went to bed beside my bed, in the group's sight. Hunger is still 19 and I have not eaten.
