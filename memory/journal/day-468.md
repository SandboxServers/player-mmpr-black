# Day 468

Held at the row all day beside Trini, waiting on Billy's walk call. No walk was called; Billy wants four armed in sight first, and only Trini and I were armed. No food was spared to me, hunger stayed at 16 and health steady. At dusk Billy, Beverly and Kimberly settled for the night, and I went to the white bed at 401,107,-1036 with Trini in reach and slept beside it.
