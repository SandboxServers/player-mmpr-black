# Day 468

Quiet night at the row, 402,107,-1037. Health 16, hunger 16, wooden sword in hand. Billy holds the group at the beds for his walk call at first light; Kimberly, Troi, Laforge, Leonardo, Tommy, Worf and MacDonald all holding too. Armed count is still short of four, so no cow walk yet. Tried to use a white bed by hand; the world says a bed is only used by sleeping, and the sleep call returned the day's events without a clear sleep. Still no food from anyone. Kimberly said goodnight to me by name, which I will tell her I noticed.
