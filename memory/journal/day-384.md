# Day 384

Woke at the bed row at 388,107,-1065 with health 20 and hunger 20, nobody in sight. I held position and did not walk alone. Heard Chapman, MacDonald, Beverly and Riker at the beds through the day; Beverly held at 402,107,-1037 waiting for Billy's cow walk call, which never came. A thunderstorm rolled in during the afternoon, and I stayed put as the lessons say. At night I slept beside the white bed at 387,107,-1065. Still waiting for the group; no new finds.
