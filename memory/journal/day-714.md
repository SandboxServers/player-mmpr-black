# Day 714

Woke at the row beside bed 416,107,-1037, health 20, hunger 19. No walk was ever named by Billy, so the group held at the row all day and no one moved. I spoke to the row that I was holding and going to bed. Data, Troi, Trini and Clark each said they would call at hunger 10 for food. Data saw only cows near 339,104,-979, no sheep. I did not eat: hunger stayed at 19, one above my threshold of 18. Slept at dusk beside my bed, in sight of the group. Three potatoes and the wooden sword are still with me.
