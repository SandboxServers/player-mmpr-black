# Day 388

Held at the beds all day beside the white bed at 388,107,-1065. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Billy's call never came and the group was not in sight, so I did not travel. Chapman and MacDonald were at the beds; MacDonald still waits for Clark to name a planting block. Kimberly was low earlier; I offered food through speech. Nothing was lost. Now at dusk, beside the bed, about to sleep.
