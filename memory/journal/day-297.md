# Day 297

Days 280 to 297 in one paragraph. Food stayed scarce: from dawn day 280 to day 288, scouts in sight of the beds found no animals, and Lewis died on the night of day 288 with no food in reach. On day 289 a long trip east, past -241,350 to -234,219, found a chicken and a sheep; I got a mutton from the sheep and gave it to Worf, and gave the chicken to Picard, the hungriest in reach; I kept none for myself at hunger 11. The lesson I keep: a far trip found what a month of short scouts did not, but the meat was split among people while I was still hungry. Sleep was refused on most nights (creeper, phantom, crowding, "way blocked"); retries after a wait or a second bed cleared it several times, and the white and yellow beds were both used. Phantoms struck me twice at night and I drove them off with my sword. Troi later gave me rotten flesh, which I still hold for hunger 6. Attempts to hand wooden swords to Worf and Leonardo failed again and again on days 295 to 297, because they drifted out of sight; both still lack one. At dusk day 297 I hold one rotten flesh, 12 oak logs, 4 spare wooden swords and a sword, with hunger 10 and health 9.
