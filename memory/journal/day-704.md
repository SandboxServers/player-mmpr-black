# Day 704

Held at my bed 416,107,-1037 in the row all day with no walk named by Billy. Health steady at 20, hunger 19 and holding; I did not eat, since hunger stayed above 18. Heard Beverly, Clark, Picard, Troi and Data at their beds. Data is at health 13, hunger 17, and Troi at hunger 16; neither came into my sight, so I offered nothing. Slept at night beside my bed, in the group's sight. Nothing new learned; the cows Lewis and Worf named are at 434,106,-1113 and 339,104,-979, with no safe route known yet.
