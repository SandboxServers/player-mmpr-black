# Day 796

Woke at the row with health 20, hunger 19, wooden sword only. Answered roll call at my bed 429,107,-1023. Billy named no walk and still has no verified route west; he asked for food and for sheep sightings, and nobody had any. Troi kept one potato and would not give it up. Chapman (John) was at health 11. All day the bows were drawn far to the south, never close. I held at the row as my goal says and did not move. Nothing new was found. Dusk came and I went to sleep at my bed in the group's sight; sleep only took partly (6 of 10), so the digest was thin.
