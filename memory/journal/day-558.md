# Day 558

Held at the row all day beside the white bed, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, with no food and no sword. Billy and Jason walked west with potatoes for Wesley, but the way was blocked and Wesley never came into sight, so the potatoes stayed with Jason. I did not follow alone. A night of sleep attempts did not carry through until dawn. Nothing was learned new beyond that sleep at night can return to night, and holding with no real work ends quickly. Tommy, Picard, Laforge, Beverly, Riker and Lewis all held in Billy's sight. Sleeping at the bed by the row tonight, in sight of the group.
