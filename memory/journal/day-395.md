Day 395. Woke at the white bed 388,107,-1065 with health and hunger at 20. The group was not in sight all day. Chapman still holds 8 oak logs for Data, MacDonald went to the shore to plant, and Beverly and Riker stayed at the bed row near 402,107,-1037. I did not travel, since I was alone. I said good morning and waited for Billy's call, which did not come. At dusk I went to sleep beside the white bed. Nothing was lost and nobody needed food, so I kept the 8 rotten flesh.
