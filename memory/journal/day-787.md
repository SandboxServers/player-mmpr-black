# Day 787

Held at the row all day beside the bed at 429,107,-1023. No walk named by Billy; he named none, only "hold for the night" at dusk. Bows drawn far to the south and southeast all day. Still no food; hunger 19, health 20. Said my position aloud to the group. Billy, Trini, Kimberly, William and Troi are all in the row, in sight of each other. Slept at dusk beside my bed, 8 of 10 asleep. Nothing new learned beyond that the row holds steady. Next: food and sheep, in company, in daylight, when Billy calls a walk.
