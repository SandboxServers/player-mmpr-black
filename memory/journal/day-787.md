# Day 787

Held at the row all day beside my bed at 429,107,-1023 and 430,107,-1023. No walk named by Billy. Bows drawn far to the south and southeast all day. Health steady at 20, hunger 19, no food, wooden sword only. Trini, Clark, MacDonald, Troi, Chapman and Beverly all held too. No sheep seen by anyone. Slept at dusk in the row's sight.
