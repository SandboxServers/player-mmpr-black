# Day 654

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1064, health 20, hunger 19, four potatoes. Held in the row all day for Billy's call. Billy named an east walk to x=460 for Billy, Deanna, Kimberly, Lewis and Picard; I was not named, so I stayed put and said so to Billy. The route was checked, nothing seen, and everyone went to bed at dusk in sight of each other. Thunder at night. Slept at my bed in Billy's sight. Nothing new learned about sheep or the south bows. Tommy and MacDonald still the weakest; Kimberly holds the potato for hunger 6 or less.
