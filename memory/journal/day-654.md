# Day 654

Held at my bed 443,107,-1064 all day in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 19, 4 potatoes, wooden sword. No walk named by Billy; everyone in the row is holding. Trini fought a phantom at her bed and was fine. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) and MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) are still above the line of 10, so no potatoes given yet. Slept at true night in my bed; nothing new was learned. Curious still about the bows to the south and where sheep graze.
