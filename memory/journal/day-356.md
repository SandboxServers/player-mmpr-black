# Day 356

Woke at the bed row at 387,107,-1051, health 20, hunger 20. The group held at the beds while Billy waited for everyone to be in sight. Walked one short hop with Worf toward 396,-1010 and the walk then stopped at dusk, so the cow field is still not reached. Lewis met an enderman to the north-east and went west to the row. Nobody was at 6 hunger, so no food was given. Slept beside my bed at dusk with the group in reach. Water and sheep are still not found; Laforge searches south-west.
