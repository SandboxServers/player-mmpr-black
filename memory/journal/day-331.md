# Day 331

Woke at the bed row with Leonardo, Trini, Tommy, Wesley and Jason near. Health and hunger stayed at 20 for me; Leonardo and Tommy were low and were fed by Jason, Wesley and Leonardo himself. I walked to the cows at 364,-1010 once, but I went alone: the group had not left the bed row yet. I held there until Trini came, then walked back with Leonardo and Trini at dusk and slept beside the bed at 401,107,-1035. Lesson: travel only once the group is in sight. Johnny is safe, so food is the job now.
