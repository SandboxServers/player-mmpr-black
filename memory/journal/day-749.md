# Day 749

Held at the row all day beside the beds, in sight of Jason, Billy, MacDonald, Worf and Wesley. No walk was named and no verified route west exists yet. Hunger stayed at 19, so I kept both potatoes for the rule of eating at 18 or below. Nothing was gained or lost. Tomorrow: hold for Billy's call, tell Billy and Kimberly first, and eat when hunger reaches 18.
