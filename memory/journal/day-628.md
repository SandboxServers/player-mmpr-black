# Day 628

Held at my bed in the row all day with the group in sight. No walk was named for me, and Billy never called one. Sleep at dusk failed once because it was not yet night; it worked at true night. MacDonald reported 5 potatoes, so my 2 owed potatoes were not needed yet; I still hold 4. Hunger stayed at 19, health 20. Nothing dangerous came near. Tommy, Picard, Laforge, Beverly, Chapman and Troi all held quietly too.
