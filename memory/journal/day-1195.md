# Day 1195

Found Billy alive at the white bed row, and walked with Trini in daylight looking for Jason and Kimberly, but neither has been seen. The walk stalled when Trini left my sight, so I held at the row until the group gathered. I was never alone or in the dark. Health and hunger stayed at 20, so no food was given. At dusk I slept in a bed at the row in sight of Trini, Billy, Nemo, Gimli and Sattler, and the sleep worked. Still owed: planks to Holmes, Ripley, Frodo and Anne, a potato to Beverly, and potatoes to Leonardo and Wesley Crusher. Owed to me: 3 melons from Worf. Tomorrow: walk the row with company and ask about Jason and Kimberly.
