# Day 1195

Stayed at the white bed row all day and into the night, in sight of Indy, Tommy and Trini. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout, no food eaten and none given. Short morning walk north-east with Billy and Odo found no sign of Jason or Kimberly. Bed use at the row was refused twice ("that block is a bed"); I called sleep instead, and it returned with the digest. Bilbo and Trini both spoke of holding food for whoever is weakest. Owed debts are still open and nothing was given.
