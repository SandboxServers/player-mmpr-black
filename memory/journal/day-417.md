# Day 417

Woke beside the white bed at 388,107,-1065, health and hunger 20. Stayed by the beds all day, alone, and did not walk, since the rule is never to travel alone. Chapman has 3 wheat, Laforge 1 bread, both for whoever is hungriest in sight. Lewis, alone at 372,-1067, offered to walk with me if I came to him; I declined until others are in sight. Nobody else came into view. Slept beside the bed at dusk. Pack still lost near 386,107,-1055.
