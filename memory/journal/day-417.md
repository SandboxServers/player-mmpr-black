# Day 417

Woke at the bed row 388,107,-1065 on day 416, health and hunger 20, empty-handed. Called my position in chat and waited most of the day. Nobody came into sight. Heard Laforge and Chapman holding bread and wheat for whoever is hungry, and Lewis calling for a walk to a bed, but I did not move, since I walk only in company. Dusk came on day 417, and I slept beside the bed at 388,107,-1065. Nothing was lost today; I was safe the whole time. Still missing: company, food, and the pack near 386,107,-1055.
