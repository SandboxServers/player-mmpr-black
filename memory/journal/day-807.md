# Day 807

Woke at the row beside bed 429,107,-1036, hunger 19, health 20, two potatoes held for Worf. Billy named no walk all day, so I held still with the group, said roll call, and watched Worf's potato wait for hunger 10. Trini heard bows to the southeast. Dusk came, I went to the bed at 429,107,-1036 with MacDonald and slept in the row's sight. Nothing new was found; the group is still waiting on Billy for the potatoes, the route west, and the sheep.
