# Day 807

Held at the row all day with no walk named. Answered MacDonald's roll call at dawn. Worf asked for my 2 potatoes; I told him I will not walk alone and will hand them over when he is in my sight or Billy calls the walk. Trini heard bows to the southeast. Slept at bed 429,107,-1036 in the row's sight at dusk; 5 of 10 asleep. Health 20, hunger 19, unarmed, 2 potatoes held. Still waiting on Billy's named walk and a verified route.
