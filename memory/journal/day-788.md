# Day 788

Held at the row all day at bed 429,107,-1023 with Billy, Trini, Kimberly, William, Troi, Beverly and MacDonald. Billy named no walk; he holds until a route is verified. No food found, hunger 19, health 20. Bows drawn far to the south and southeast the whole day. I answered Billy's question about Data and cows earlier: I have seen neither myself. Slept at dusk beside my bed in the row's sight.
