# Day 471

Stayed at the row all day and night by the white beds at 387,107,-1037 and -1036, in Billy's sight. Pillagers came near at one point and sleep was refused until they left. A wooden sword I had in my pack went to Tommy by mistake and then to Leonardo; I still do not know how it moves between packs, so I should not rely on handing swords around. Sleep finally worked at the bed (8 of 10 asleep). No walk, no food spared to me yet; hunger stands at 16. Lesson: the group holds together through the night, and sleeping beside the bed in their sight is safe enough.
