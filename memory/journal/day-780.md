# Day 780

Held at the row all day beside my bed at 443,107,-1037 in the group's sight. Billy named no walk. Hunger stayed at 19 with no food anywhere in sight; health steady at 20. Chapman (health 11, hunger 17), MacDonald, Trini, Data and Deanna are the ones hungry and hurt. Heard a bow to the southwest and Trini saw bows drawn to the south-east. No sheep seen by anyone; cows only near 339,104,-979. Slept at night by my bed in the row's sight.
