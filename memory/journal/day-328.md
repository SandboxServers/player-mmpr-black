# Day 328

Morning at the north-east bed row with Tommy, Leonardo and Jason. A zombie came at Jason and Troi, and Jason was brought down to health 1 before he got back to the west bed. Zack and Tommy went to help, and Leonardo brought melon. Jason recovered to health 20. Then a skeleton shot at the group from the north-west, and we fell back to 410,-1030 and held together. In the late afternoon the group walked to the beds at 401,-1037 and slept together at dusk. Johnny and Kimberly are still missing. The melon piles at 411,-1055 and 302,80,-822 wait for daylight and company. Troi was last seen at health 3 or 4, running west from a zombie, and is not known to be safe.
