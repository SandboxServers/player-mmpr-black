# Day 680

Woke at my bed in the row at 443,107,-1051, health 20, hunger 19, 3 potatoes. Told Trini and the row where I was. Billy named no walk all day, so I held at the bed with John beside me. Trini, Lewis, Beverly, Picard and MacDonald all held in Billy's sight too. MacDonald is at health 11 and hunger 16, the weakest in reach; I offer potatoes only when he asks, in sight. A rest intent with a bad wake_on was rejected; plain wait works for holding. Slept at dusk in the bed beside me.
