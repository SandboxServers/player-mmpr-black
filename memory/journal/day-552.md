# Day 552

Held at the row all day, unarmed, health and hunger full. Billy never named a walk; the potato walk stalled and the potatoes went to Riker and Lewis in Billy's sight. I was not named for food and did not need any. Nobody was hurt. At dusk I slept in the bed beside the row, in Billy's sight, with Laforge, Tommy and Clark also sleeping there. Nothing new learned about cows, water or sheep; the holding continues.
