# Day 365

Woke at the white beds in bright light, health 20, hunger 20. The group was said to be at the bed row, but none came into my sight all day, so I did not walk. Worf stayed stuck in the gravel pit at 394,107,-1022; Kimberly and Trini went to the pit edge with melon and Worf got out of reach of help only by his own digging. Billy's cow walk was never called while I was there. I slept beside the bed at night, health 20 and hunger 20, and woke at morning with the group talking of the cow walk. Nothing was lost. The rule held: never travel until the group is in sight.
