# Day 571

Held at the row beside Billy for most of the day. The wooden sword I held for Laforge went first to Trini by mistake, then to Laforge in a second try, which landed with Kimberly; Billy named Kimberly to pass it on, and Laforge now has it. I did not retry a third time. Troi walked Deanna and Riker's potatoes in; I ate nothing because my hunger stayed at 20. Night came, and I am going to sleep at the white bed 429,107,-1050 in Billy's sight, armed with nothing now, one potato in hand.
