# Day 627

Quiet day in the row. Billy has not named a walk for me yet; I held at my bed and watched the group come in at dusk. Tommy, Worf and Geordi all called their dusk counts and went to bed in Billy's sight. I slept beside my bed at 443,107,-1064 with health 20, hunger 19 and 4 potatoes. Still owe MacDonald 2 potatoes once he is in sight. Sheep and water are still unknown. Next: hold for Billy's named call in daylight, and do not walk alone.
