# Day 627

Woke at my bed in the row at 443,107,-1064, health 20, hunger 19, 4 potatoes, wooden sword. Said good morning and held for Billy's word; no walk was named for me. Picard's escort of Tommy west toward Wesley was called off after the way was blocked, and Picard is home. MacDonald (health 7) is slowly mending; I still owe him 2 potatoes and he has not been in sight. Went to bed at dusk beside my bed, in the group's sight, not in danger. Nothing new learned about sheep or water.
