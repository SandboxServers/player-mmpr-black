# Day 758

Held at the row all day beside bed 416,107,-1037, in sight of Billy, Jason, Worf, Wesley, John and Leonardo. Billy named no walk: no verified route west past x=370 and no sheep seen. Cows reported near 339,104,-979 by Data. MacDonald (health 15, hunger 16) and Data (health 13, hunger 17) are the weakest; William offered MacDonald five potatoes at hunger 10. My hunger stayed at 19, so I kept my two potatoes. Slept at night beside my bed in the group's sight.
