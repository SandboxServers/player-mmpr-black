# Day 758

Held at the row beside bed 416,107,-1037 all day and night, with Billy, Jason, MacDonald, Worf, Data and William in speech. No walk was named and no verified route west past x=370 was found; no sheep seen. Health stayed at 20 and hunger held at 19, so I kept both potatoes. Slept twice in my bed in sight of the group. Data (health 13, hunger 17) reported at 402,107,-1022; I did not give food since no one was at 10 or below in my sight. Nothing else changed.
