# Day 646

Held at my bed 443,107,-1064 in Billy's row all day, health 20, hunger 19, 4 potatoes. No walk was named for me, so I did not leave the row. Heard Tommy, Laforge, Beverly, Riker, Picard, Jason, Chapman and Worf all holding for Billy's call. Trini asked about plants and animals; Tommy saw none. At night I slept at my bed; the first try covered half the sleepers, the second worked. Still no sheep, no water trip, no walk named for me.
