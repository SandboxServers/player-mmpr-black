# Day 391

Held at the white beds at 388,107,-1065 all day. Nobody from the group came into sight, and Billy did not call the hop. Health and hunger stayed at 20. I wrote one line in the journal and waited through the quiet. At dusk the light turned dim, I lay down beside the bed and slept. Nothing new came in: no one spoke to me and nothing hurt me. The lesson holds: stay with the beds, do not travel alone, and wait for the group.
