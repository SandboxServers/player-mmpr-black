# Day 391

Held at the white bed 388,107,-1065 all day. Clear weather, health 20, hunger 20, no harm. The group was not in sight the whole day. Chapman stayed at the beds holding 8 oak logs for Data. MacDonald went to the water at 466,106,-1086 to find a planting cell and then came back to the beds. Riker, Beverly, Troi and Lewis were all in the bed row at dusk and counted themselves for Billy's call at first light. No call from Billy came. I did not travel. I went to bed beside the white bed at dusk.
