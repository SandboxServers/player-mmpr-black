# Day 808

Held at the row all day beside the white beds. No walk named by Billy. Worf has not been seen; I still hold 2 potatoes for him. Hunger stayed at 19, health 20. Heard that a bow was drawn to the southwest, so no walking. John is at 11 health and hunger 17, the most in need. Slept at dusk beside the white bed at 443,107,-1037, in Billy's sight.
