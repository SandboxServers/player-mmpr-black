# Handoff, Zack Taylor, day 389 (night)

- Beside the white bed at 388,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20. Not in danger.
- Slept at dusk; no errand out. Group not in sight. MacDonald went to the shore farm, Chapman still at the beds with 8 oak logs for Data.
- Next: stay at the beds, wait for Billy's call at first light. Never travel alone. Give food to the lowest (8 rotten flesh held).
