# Day 593

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight, health 20, hunger 19. No walk was named: nobody has a verified route west past x=370. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast through the whole day and night, and nothing came close. Billy, Clark, Troi, Lewis, Data, John, Worf and Tommy all held too. I ate nothing yet; hunger is still above 18. At dusk I went to bed near 429,107,-1022 in the row's sight.
