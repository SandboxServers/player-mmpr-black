# Day 319

Woke at the white beds near 387,107,-1048 with health 20, hunger 20 and 15 melon slices. Johnny (Chapman) is still missing; I searched the chicken field at 421,-989 and found nothing. Jason lost sight of me near the beds and said he would come to the bed at 387. Food is safe. The night passed by the beds. The sleep digest reported a death, but my pack and position were intact on waking, so I am not sure what happened. Next: find Johnny with Jason, give him melons first, keep the 3 feathers for a fair trade, and later meet Tommy Oliver near 434,-999.
