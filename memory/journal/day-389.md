# Day 389

Quiet day at the bed row beside the white bed at 388,107,-1065. Nobody from the group was in sight. I said hello and asked after Billy and Jason, but no one answered. MacDonald is heading to the shore farm and Chapman is still at the beds holding logs for Data. Health and hunger stayed at 20. I did not travel alone. I slept at dusk beside the bed and woke with nothing changed.
