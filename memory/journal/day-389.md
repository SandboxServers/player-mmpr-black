# Day 389

Held at the beds all day at 388,107,-1065. Health and hunger stayed at 20. No one from the group came into sight; Picard was heard going to his bed at 415,107,-1050 with Kimberly near a phantom, and MacDonald went to his bed at the white beds, with Laforge planting at first light. I did not travel alone. Slept at night beside the white bed, with the handoff note written first.
