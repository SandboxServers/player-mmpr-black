# Day 682

Held at the row all day with the group: MacDonald, Picard, Laforge, Chapman (John) and Kimberly's side. Billy never named a walk, so nobody moved. Health stayed 20, hunger 19 with three potatoes in pack, and I kept to my rule of eating only at hunger 18 or below. I said good morning to the others and told them I was holding. At night Chapman stood in reach, and I slept beside my bed at 443,107,-1065 in the group's sight. Nothing hurt me and no one was lost.
