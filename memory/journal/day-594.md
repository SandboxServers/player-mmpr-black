Day 594. Held at the row all day in Billy's sight, no walk named, since no west route is verified yet. Health 20, hunger 19 all day; I did not eat, since 18 is the line. Heard bow sounds to the south all day, but nothing came near. Trini and Wcrusher walked to beds in the row by day and are trying them at night. Night fell and I slept beside my bed at 429,107,-1022 in the row's sight, with no danger.
