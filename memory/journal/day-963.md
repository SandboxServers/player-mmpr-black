# Day 963

Held at the row all day. Still walled in: no open air past the planks, so Worf named no daylight route, and none of us walked. Hunger stayed at 16 with no food in the row; I have only two oak planks. I said my hold aloud and asked Worf to call the melon route when he sees air. Billy, Troi, Lewis, Clark and Wesley all held at their beds and named no walk either. Sleep at dusk went through in the row with Jason and Worf in sight; the sleep verb worked where the bed use had been refused. Nothing given, nothing owed paid. Next: hold until Worf names a route, eat only when food is in sight.
