Day 329. Dusk, then night at the white beds at 401,107,-1037. Jason, Tommy, Wesley and Picard stayed near in sight. A skeleton shot Tommy near the pile; we pulled back to the beds together. Items at 413,-1054 were gone when we got there. We did not reach Kimberly before dark. Slept in the bed row with the group. Lesson holds: be at the beds before dusk, and move only in sight of each other.
