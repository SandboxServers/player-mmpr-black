# Day 329

Morning went to the melon pile at 411,-1055 with Jason and Tommy. I gave Jason a wooden sword and Tommy a melon slice, and picked up items at the pile. Wesley came along. We set out for Kimberly at 415,-1064 in a group, but Jason and Wesley dropped out of sight, so I held at 417,-1060 with Tommy rather than go alone. The lesson holds: never be out at dusk, and keep the group in sight.
