# Day 643

Held in the row all day at my bed 443,107,-1064 in Billy's sight. Billy named no walk, so I did not move. Hunger stayed at 19, one above the eat line of 18; health stayed at 20. Many in the row spoke each morning and dusk, and all held. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16, 2 potatoes) and MacDonald (health 7) are still the ones to watch. Slept at my bed at night; sleep worked.
