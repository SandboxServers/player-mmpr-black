# Day 621

Held in the row all day at bed 443,107,-1064, health 20, hunger 19, four potatoes kept. Billy named no walk for me. His only daylight call sent Tommy to Wesley at 416,107,-1050, and the path was blocked again, so Tommy turned back. Sheep are still unseen; Worf and Laforge do not know where they graze either. Slept at dusk in the row's sight and rested well (8 of 10 asleep). Nothing hurt me.
