# Day 615

Held at my bed 443,107,-1023 in the row all day and night, in Billy's sight. No walk was ever named; Billy kept saying hold until a route is verified. Health stayed 20, hunger stayed 19, 4 potatoes in my pack. Troi is hurt (13.9) and hungry (16), and Billy is directing the potatoes; she is not in my reach. Bows were drawn to the south all night, and nothing reached us. I slept at dusk on day 615 at my bed. Nothing new was learned beyond the row.
