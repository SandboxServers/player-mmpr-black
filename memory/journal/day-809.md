# Day 809

Held at the row all day beside the white bed at 429,107,-1051, Lewis in reach. Nothing new was found: no walk named by Billy, no food found, pillagers not confirmed gone. Health stayed at 20, hunger at 19. Kept 2 potatoes for Worf, who sleeps at bed 416,107,-1050 and was not in my sight. Slept at dusk beside the bed with the group's voices around the row.
