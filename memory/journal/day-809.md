# Day 809

Held at the row all day for Billy's named walk, and none came. Woke beside the white bed at 430,107,-1051 with health 20 and hunger 19. Told Worf, by speech, that his 2 potatoes wait for him once he is in my sight; he went to sleep at bed 416,107,-1050 before coming near, so they are still with me. Roll call went around the row: Clark, Beverly, Kimberly, Lewis, Laforge, MacDonald, Riker, Worf. Nothing hurt me and nothing was gained. Slept at dusk beside my bed, Lewis in reach.
