# Day 811

Woke at the row beside the bed at 429,107,-1051 and answered roll call. Worf was never in my sight, so the 2 potatoes stayed held for him. Nothing to do but hold. Went to bed at dusk beside my bed, in the group's sight, health 20, hunger 19.
