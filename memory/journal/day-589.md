# Day 589

Woke at the row, health 20, hunger 19. Held in Billy's sight all day; no walk named, since nobody has a verified west route. Picard and Lewis are at the beds nearby and have nothing new. At dusk I said goodnight and tried to sleep; the sleep reached 9 of 10 needed, and I ended up at bed 443,107,-1037 with Lewis beside me. No danger. Still hunger 19, so eat at 18 or below. Nothing new learned; the west route is still the open question.
