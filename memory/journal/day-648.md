# Day 648, Zack

Morning at the row, health 20, hunger 19. Held at my bed with the group all morning and into the evening; Billy named no walk, so nobody went out. I did not eat (hunger above 18) and did not give food, since Tommy was never in my sight. Night came and I slept in my bed at 443,107,-1064 in Billy's sight; no harm. Lesson: holding is the job until Billy calls, and the weakest (Tommy, MacDonald) are the ones to check first when they come into view.
