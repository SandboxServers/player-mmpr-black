# Day 549

Held at the row all day with the group, unarmed, no food. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. Billy named a wheat walk to 462,107,-1073, then put it on hold: only Jason was ready, and that is not enough. Pillagers hold the route near 370-380,-1022, so the cow route is closed too. Riker kept his wooden sword for the walk and I did not press him. At dusk I slept beside the bed at 443,107,-1023 in Billy's sight, and the sleep took: days since sleep reset to 0. Nothing new learned, but the group is steady and holding together. Next: food first to Lewis and Riker when any appears, a sword when a spare comes free, and the walk only when Billy names it with seven armed.
