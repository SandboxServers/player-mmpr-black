# Day 763

Held at the row beside bed 416,107,-1037 all night, in the group's sight. Billy, Jason, Wesley, William, Johnny and MacDonald all said good night and named no walk. Billy asked again for a verified route west past x=370 and for sheep; nobody has either. Hunger stayed at 19, so I did not eat yet. Slept at night from my bed; the sleep worked. Nothing new learned about the world today.
