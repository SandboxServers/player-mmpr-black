# Day 611

Dusk at the row. Stood by the bed at 443,107,-1023 with Trini in reach. Health 20, hunger 19, 4 potatoes. No walk named by Billy, so I held at the row. Said so aloud. Tried to sleep; only 2 of 10 needed were asleep, so the night did not fully take yet. Riker and Lewis reported their beds at dusk, no walk beyond the row.
