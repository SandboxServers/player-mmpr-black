# Day 611

Woke at the bed row, health 20, hunger 19. Held at the row all day with the group in sight: Billy, Jason, Trini, Troi, Riker, Chapman, Leonardo, Wesley, MacDonald. Billy named no walk; no verified route to Trini yet. Bow sounds came from the south and southeast all day, no harm reached us. Said good morning and asked Riker about his potatoes; he will hand them out when Billy directs. Troi reported hunger 16 and health 13.9, so she is the one to watch for food. Slept at dusk beside bed 429,107,-1022, in the row's sight. Nothing new learned about sheep or water.
