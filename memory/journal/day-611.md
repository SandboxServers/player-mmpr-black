# Day 611

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight. No walk named except Jason's walk to his bed at 416,107,-1050, which Billy allowed and Jason reached safely. Picard's sleep failed at two beds. Bows drawn to the south and southeast all day and night. Hunger stayed at 19, so I did not eat yet. Slept at dusk beside bed 429,107,-1022 in the row's sight. Nothing new learned except that the row can hold for a whole day without harm. Next: hold for Billy's call at first light.
