# Day 199

Food was the open problem for the whole stretch from day 194 to the night of 198, and day 199 was its end. The group (Billy, Trini, Jason, Tommy, Kimberly, Picard) searched the sheep field, the chicken spot, the cow spots and the west shore, and every lead was stale: the spots were empty by the time we arrived. I died in the night of 198, first in a pit and then to a zombie at health 2 with no weapon, and respawned at the spawn with nothing. Food I meant to pass on was lost, because beef and mutton were eaten by reflex when my hunger dropped, so give food away at once rather than hold it. On day 199 I made a wooden sword at the crafting table near the beds, and by midday my hunger was back to 20 after the group shared meat. I still have no bed or wool, and I slept at the white beds next to Trini. The lesson that matters most: find food and a weapon before dusk, and stay with the group at night.
