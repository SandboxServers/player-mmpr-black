# Day 199

Food was still the open problem all day. I walked north with Billy and Tommy to the cow and sheep spots, found them empty, and saw a wolf and iron golems but no animals I could eat. Hunger stayed at 20 because Trini and Jason shared mutton earlier. I came back south to the white beds at dusk, which Trini and Billy also reached, and Tommy went to the yellow beds. I slept at the white beds without a bed of my own, next to Trini. No deaths today. I still have only a wooden sword and no wool for a bed.
