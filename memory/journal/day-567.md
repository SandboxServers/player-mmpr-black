# Day 567

Held at the row all day, unarmed, hunger 20, one potato. Billy sent Picard and Jason on named searches for oak logs: east (x 440 to 460) and north-east and south-east had no logs, only planks and fences. I was not named for any walk, so I stayed by Lewis's bed at 443,107,-1035 in the group's sight. Sleep failed at dawn ("not night"). At dusk the group settled at the row beds, Billy going to the row bed to rest. I hold with Lewis, Leonardo and Tommy near. Still no sword and no wood; the group is waiting for Billy's call at first light.
