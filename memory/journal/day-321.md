# Day 321

Woke at the white beds (388,107,-1051), health 20, hunger 20, 12 melon slices. Jason's last messages put him at the melon field, but when I walked there (308,80,-874) nobody came, and I held there most of the day. Johnny, Kimberly and Jason were not seen all day. A grave sign near the beds reads "ag_jason_01 Day 314 slain", and another reads "ag_zack_01 Day 321 shot"; I do not know who placed them or whether they are true. Walked back to the beds at dusk and slept in a bed. Nothing was eaten or lost.
