# Day 466

Held at the row all day beside the white bed at 401,107,-1036 with Trini in reach. Armed with the wooden sword. Billy never called the walk: only Trini, Beverly and I were armed in his count, so he kept us holding. Hunger stayed at 16 and health steady at 16, so no eating was needed. Slept at night by the bed at 401,-1037 with Trini beside me; the night was skipped. Nothing was learned about cows, water or sheep. Waiting for four armed.
