# Day 731

Held at the row beside my bed at 416,107,-1037 all day, in the group's sight, waiting for Billy's named walk. None came. Billy, Troi and Worf each said goodnight at the row with no walk named. Health stayed at 20. Hunger sat at 19, one above the potato line of 18, so I kept both potatoes. I said goodnight at the row and slept. Nothing new was learned today. Next: Billy's walk, told to Billy and Kimberly first; a potato at hunger 18 or below; Troi gets a potato at hunger 10 if she stands in my sight.
