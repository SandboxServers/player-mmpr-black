# Day 650

Held in the row with the group through the dusk. Billy named no walk. Tommy, Lewis, Trini, MacDonald and Worf all said goodnight in Billy's sight. I slept in my bed at 443,107,-1064 and the sleep worked. Health 20, hunger 19, 4 potatoes, wooden sword. Nothing new learned about sheep or the bows to the south. Next at waking: hold for Billy's call, eat at hunger 18 or below, do not walk alone.
