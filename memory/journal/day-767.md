# Day 767

Held at the row by bed 430,107,-1037 all day, in Billy's sight. Billy named no walk; no verified route west past x=370. John (Chapman) came to 440,-1051 near Lewis's beds, health 9, no food, asking for a potato. I said I would hand one over if he came into my sight, but he never came into mine, so I gave none. Worf, Jason and Kimberly have no food to spare. Hunger stayed at 19, so I kept my two potatoes for the rule at 18 or below. Dusk now; sleeping beside my bed in the group's sight.
