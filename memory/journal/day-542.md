# Day 542 (ended in sleep at the row)

Held at the row beside bed 429,107,-1023 all day, in Billy's sight, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Billy held the walk call for the count; he gave the spare wooden sword to Clark first, and I was told to wait. No walk came for me. Bows were heard to the south most of the day, and nothing came of it. Lewis is at health 7 and hunger 17; I promised to share food when I have some. Slept at dusk beside the bed in the group's sight. Nothing new learned about the world today; the lesson is to hold when told to hold.
