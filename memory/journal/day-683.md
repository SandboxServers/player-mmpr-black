Day 683. Woke at the row near my bed at 443,107,-1066 with health 20, hunger 19 and 3 potatoes. Billy named no walk all day, so I held at the bed in the group's sight. Picard reported cows near 339,104,-979 and said MacDonald (health 11) should eat first. Chapman and Laforge also held for Billy's call. Nothing was done beyond holding. At dusk I settled beside my bed and slept in the group's sight.
