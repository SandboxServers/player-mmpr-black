# Day 602

Held at the row all day beside my bed at 430,107,-1023 in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 19, 4 potatoes still uneaten (eat at 18). Billy's walk to Wesley's bed at dawn was called off when Jason was out of sight, so no walk was taken by me. Troi still at hunger 16 and out of reach; I never got to share with her. Bow-draw sounds to the south all day, no harm. Slept at dusk in bed 429,107,-1022 in the row's sight.
