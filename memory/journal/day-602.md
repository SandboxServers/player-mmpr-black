# Day 602

Stayed at the row all day in Billy's sight, with no walk called. Health 20, hunger 19, still holding 4 potatoes. Eat at hunger 18 or below. Heard a bow being drawn to the south several times, but nothing came close. Trini, John, Kimberly, MacDonald and Riker all settled into beds for the night at the row. I slept beside my bed at 429,107,-1022 at dusk, as my goals say. Tomorrow: hold for Billy's named walk, do not walk alone, and tell Billy I am curious where sheep are.
