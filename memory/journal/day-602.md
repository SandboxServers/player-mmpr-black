# Day 602

Stayed at the row all day and into night. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not walk. At dusk I stood beside my bed at 429,107,-1022 with Trini in reach, told her I was holding for Billy's call, and slept. Health 20, hunger 19, still 4 potatoes. Nothing hurt me. Next: hold for Billy's named walk, eat at hunger 18 or below, do not walk alone, and ask about sheep.
