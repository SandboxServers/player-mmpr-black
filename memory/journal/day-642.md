# Day 642

Held at my bed 443,107,-1064 in the row all day with health 20 and hunger 19; I never ate because hunger stayed above 18. Billy named no walk. Dusk came and the row slept in his sight, and I slept at my bed at true night. This morning Billy reported bows to the south, reported by Troi, and said no walk until they see it themselves. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) and MacDonald (health 7, hunger 16) are still the ones to watch; MacDonald has six potatoes and has said he will eat when his hunger allows. Nobody has seen sheep yet. Nothing was learned new today beyond the bows report.
