# Day 364

Thunder in the morning, then clear. Stayed at the white beds 388,107,-1051 with the group in sight all day. Health and hunger held at 20. Billy's cow-walk call did not come before I slept. Worf built a plank shelter by the beds and learned that a bed is used by standing beside it, not by clicking it. Trini and Kimberly are low and hold melon and chicken for the weakest. Water and sheep are still not found. I slept at dusk beside the bed row, with nothing half-done.
