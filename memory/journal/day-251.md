# Day 251

Food stayed the main problem for two weeks (days 238 to 251). The group searched the north, west, east and south grass and the north lake for cows, sheep, pigs and wheat and found almost nothing; the only find was one chicken on the east grass near -236,203, which went to Riker, then Data, then Trini. Hunger went between 20 and 15. A skeleton and a phantom at the north-west shore took me to 0.7 health even with a wooden sword. Then, at dawn of day 250, I went alone after a cow seen near -286,245, and an unseen attacker killed me near -279,228 while bows sounded; my pack (2 beef, leather, bone, saplings, sticks, a log, and my sword) stayed on the ground there. I came back to the white beds with nothing. Sleep was refused several times with phantoms or a creeper nearby, and worked on retries or at the yellow bed. Laforge and Wesley now hold porkchops, which I asked them to give to Worf and Trini first. Lessons: eat before hunger reaches 10, carry a weapon, never chase a lone sighting north or near bows, and give food at once, Trini first.
