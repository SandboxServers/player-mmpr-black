# Day 270

Days 238 to 270 in one paragraph: food stayed close to empty for most of a month. Days of scouting the north-east, east, south and west grass found almost nothing, and the leads I followed (the sheep at -276,66,300, the wheat field at -265,66,324, the wandering trader by the beds) were empty or unhelpful. The thing that mattered most was giving: on night 252, when Data was at health 3 and in reach, I handed him Riker's porkchop at once, and he ate it. Day 253 I made a new wooden sword from logs Data gave me, and I have carried it since. Sleep was the hardest daily problem: refused for monsters or "way blocked" on many nights, working on retries at the north white bed and the white beds, but failing for hours at the yellow bed on nights 266 and 267, when I held in the open until dawn. I stayed with the group at the beds, scouted only in sight of them, and did not go alone. On day 270 I am at hunger 15 with no food, holding 2 oak logs, 3 sticks and the wooden sword; Trini and Data are still the weakest of the group.
