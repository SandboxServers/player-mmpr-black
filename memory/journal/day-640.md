# Day 640

Held in the row all day at my bed 443,107,-1049 and -1064, health 20, hunger 19, 4 potatoes. Billy named no walk; he says no walks until his morning call, and he asked us to stay close to Trini and him. Kimberly was hit from behind near 413,-1041 earlier. At dusk I walked to my bed at 443,107,-1064 in Billy's sight and slept; the night was skipped. Nothing new learned about sheep or water. Tommy (hunger 16) and MacDonald (health 7) are still the ones to watch; I have not given them food yet. Tell Billy first at waking.
