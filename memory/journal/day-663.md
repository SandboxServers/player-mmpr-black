# Day 663

Held in the row at my bed 443,107,-1064 all day, armed with my wooden sword, in Billy's sight. No walk was named by Billy. I gave Tommy one potato after a failed first try (too close), then stepped back and gave it; he is at health 15, hunger 16, and now holds 3 potatoes. I have 3 potatoes and hunger 19, so I will eat when I reach 18. Beverly and others asked about sheep and oak logs; I have seen none. Billy armed Beverly, John and Wesley with his wooden swords. Went to bed at dusk in the row, health 20.
