# Day 400

Quiet day at the bed row near 388,107,-1065. Stayed put, did not travel, as the goals say. Health and hunger held at 20. Heard Macdonald say he was going to bed by the white bed. The group is still not in sight. Slept at night beside the bed; the sleep worked. Still holding 3 feathers for Worf, 8 rotten flesh for whoever drops to 6, and Johnny's 8 oak logs are meant for Data.
