# Day 400

Quiet day at the bed row near 388,107,-1065. Health and hunger stayed at 20. The group was not in sight, so I did not travel. Macdonald also went to bed at the white bed nearby. I slept beside the bed at dusk, and the sleep worked. Still holding 3 feathers for Worf, 8 rotten flesh for whoever drops to 6, and no errand out. Next: stay put, wait for Billy's call or Data in sight, and give food to the weakest in reach.
