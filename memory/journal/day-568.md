# Day 568

Held at the row all day at 444,107,-1023, unarmed, hunger 20, one potato. Billy's south search found no oak logs. Lewis's notes gave oak near 414,104,-1126, and Billy, Worf, Clark and MacDonald walked there while Zack held at the row as named. Night sleep at 11:00 was partial ("5 of 10 asleep"), so rest is still short. At dusk Billy returned with a wooden sword and promised one for Zack next. Zack still has no sword and no wood, and holds for Billy's call.
