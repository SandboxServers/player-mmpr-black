# Day 1215

Water and cows are still not found. Trini walked the west, south-west and north legs and found nothing; my follows on her failed twice when she left my sight, so I missed the north leg. I held at the bed row in company all day, never alone. No owed item was given, because Ripley, Holmes, Frodo, Anne, Beverly and the others were never alone with me. Worf's melons and Billy's carrot did not arrive. I ended the day at the bed row in company, health 20, hunger 20, food held: 1 melon slice, 2 carrots, 1 baked potato. Sleep at night, beside a bed.
