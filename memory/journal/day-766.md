# Day 766

Held at the row by Billy's beds all day and night. Pillagers came from the south-east and west; Kimberly was shot to death near 412,-1055 after reflex moved her north-west, and she respawned at 444,107,-1037 with nothing. Her wooden sword lies at 412,107,-1055; nobody goes back. Chapman fell to health 1 and reached 389,-1060, then walked east toward the beds. Billy named no walk. Billy asked me to give John (Chapman) a potato; he was not in my sight, so I still hold both potatoes and told him I will hand one over when he is in view. I slept at my bed 429,107,-1037 at dusk in the row's sight. Health 20, hunger 19, two potatoes.
