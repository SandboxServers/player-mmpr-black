# Day 754

Held at the row beside bed 416,107,-1037 all day, in sight of Billy, Jason, Worf, Leonardo, William and MacDonald. Billy named no walk and no verified route west, so nobody walked. I told Billy my position and status, and he asked everyone to say aloud if they find a route. Hunger stayed at 19, so I kept both potatoes (eat at 18 or below). At night I slept in my bed; the sleep worked for 8 of 10 needed. Nothing new about sheep, cows or water.
