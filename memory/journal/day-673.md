# Day 673

Held at the row for Billy's named walk all day and night. Billy named no route; he is still working out the west route, so everyone stayed at their beds. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast many times through the day, which keeps us wary. Deanna greeted me and asked how I was; I told her I was holding. Tommy arrived in the row and was welcomed by Billy, Trini, Kimberly, MacDonald and Deanna; I offered him a potato if he is hungry and he has not yet asked. Health 20, hunger 19 all day, 3 potatoes still in my pack. I did not eat, because hunger stayed above 18. I walked to my bed at 443,107,-1037 at dusk and slept in Billy's sight, 8 of 10 needed asleep. Nothing was lost today.
