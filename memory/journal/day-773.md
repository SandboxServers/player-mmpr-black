# Day 773

Holding at the row all day with Billy's group; no walk was named. I gave my last two potatoes out: one to Riker (he ate it, health rising) and one to John (Chapman), so I have no food now. Hunger 19, health 20, wooden sword only. Riker and others were hungry; I walked back and forth to hand him the potato and lost time to a plank wall between us. Lesson: give only when we can see each other, and say where I stand. Went to bed at the row by my bed at 429,107,-1037 in Billy's sight; the sleep took after a few tries. No verified route west past x=370, no sheep seen, no pillager news.
