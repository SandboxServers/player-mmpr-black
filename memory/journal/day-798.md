# Day 798

Held at the row all day with the group, no walk named by Billy, bows drawn to the south. No food found; hunger 19, health 20, wooden sword only. Roll call given at the row. Billy called night and everyone went to their beds; I slept beside my bed at 429,107,-1037, in sight of Billy, Trini and the others. Still no sheep, water or potatoes route confirmed.
