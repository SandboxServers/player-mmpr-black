# Day 462

Woke at the row beside the white bed at 416,107,-1037, health 16, hunger 16, wooden sword in hand. Told Billy to try the sword on grass or dirt. He found none in view from the row and called for four armed walkers. I said yes and held, but no walk was called before dusk. Many others (Worf, Beverly, Wesley, Tommy, Picard, Chapman) held at the row unarmed, waiting on Billy's count. Hunger never fell low enough to eat. I slept beside the white bed at 415,107,-1036 in the row's sight. Nothing new learned about cows, chickens, water or sheep yet. Next: hold for Billy's call, eat at hunger 6 or below.
