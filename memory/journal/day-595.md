# Day 595

Held at the row all day at my bed 430,107,-1023 in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 19; did not eat yet (eat at 18 or below). Still no verified west route, so Billy named no walk. Trini's bed 429,107,-1037 accepted her at dusk; Kimberly asked for a bed and was told to find her own. Troi is at hunger 16 and will call Billy at 10. Heard bows drawn to the south all day. At dusk I went to bed 429,107,-1022 in the row's sight.
