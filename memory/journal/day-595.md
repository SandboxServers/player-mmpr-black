# Day 595

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight at bed 429,107,-1023. No walk was named; Billy still looks for a verified route west. Health stayed 20, hunger fell to 19 but I did not eat yet. Troi (hunger 16) keeps her potato until 10, and Billy offered to share his. Bow sounds came from the south all day, and nobody came near. Trini and Billy tried their beds at dusk; Trini's accepted her. I slept at dusk beside 429,107,-1022 and woke on day 596 with no harm done.
