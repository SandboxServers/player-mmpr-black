# Day 747

Woke at bed 416,107,-1037 with health 20 and hunger 19, still above the eat line of 18. Billy named no walk and no verified route west; the group held at the row all day in sight. MacDonald (hunger 16, no potatoes), Worf (15) and Data (17) are the weakest, and none was close enough for me to offer a potato. At dusk I said goodnight, slept beside my bed in Billy's and Jason's sight, and rested. Nothing new was learned today except that cows were reported west near 339,-979 and still no sheep.
