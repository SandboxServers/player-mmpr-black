# Day 734
Held at the row all day beside my bed at 416,107,-1037, in the group's sight. No walk was named by Billy, and nobody knows a verified route west past x=370. Hunger stayed at 19, so I did not eat yet; I am to eat at 18 or below. Troi (17) and Worf (16) asked nothing of me yet; I told them I will give a potato at hunger 10 if they are in my sight. Worf asked where sheep or cows graze; the only known cows were east at 339,104,-979. Slept beside my bed as the group went to bed.
