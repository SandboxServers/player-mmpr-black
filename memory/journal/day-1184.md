Day 1184. Quiet day at the bed row with the group in sight. Health and hunger stayed at 20, so I ate nothing. Fogg, Lara, Marty and Atticus all went to bed here too, and Atticus offered food to Bilbo and Trini first when he has any. No endermen in view at dusk. I slept in a bed at the row; the sleep ran and the night passed. Still not found: Jason, Kimberly, Billy and the others on my list. Owed planks and potatoes still stand; I gave nothing today because no one was alone in my reach.
