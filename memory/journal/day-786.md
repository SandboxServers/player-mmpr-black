# Day 786

Held at the row all day with the group in sight, no walk named by Billy. Troi has one potato and will not go west; bows were heard far to the southwest all day. Hunger stayed at 19 with no food found, health steady at 20. At night I slept beside my bed at 443,107,-1022 with the row in sight. Nothing new learned about sheep, water or a route west.
