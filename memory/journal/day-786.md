Day 786. Held at the row all day and through the dusk, standing by the beds at 430,107,-1023 in sight of Clark, Macdonald, Chapman and Troi. Billy named no walk. Health stayed at 20, hunger at 19, and I still have no food, only the wooden sword. Heard bows drawn to the south all day, never close. A first sleep attempt took only partly (4 of 10); the second one worked. Nothing new was learned about the route west or sheep. Tomorrow: keep holding for Billy's call, and look for food once it is named.
