# Day 383

Held at the bed row all day, in the group's sight, health 20, hunger 20. Billy's cow walk was not called from my spot; I did not travel. Beverly and Kimberly were near at 402,-1037 with hunger 17 and 6 health; Kimberly declined my flesh for now. Billy went out with Data, Tommy, Picard, Lewis and Troi to count and walk him back to the group, and he is safe at 4 health. Slept at dusk beside the white bed at 387,107,-1065. Nothing hurt me today.
