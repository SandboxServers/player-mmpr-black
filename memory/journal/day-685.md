# Day 685

Woke at my bed in the row at 444,107,-1051 with health 20 and hunger 19. Held there all morning for Billy's named walk; he has still named none. The row talked in turns: Beverly, Picard, Chapman, Trini (hunger 17, will ask Billy at 10), Laforge, Lewis. Nobody was hurt. No walk was named, so I did not leave the row. At dusk I slept at my bed beside the group. Nothing new was learned today except that the group is steady and waiting.
