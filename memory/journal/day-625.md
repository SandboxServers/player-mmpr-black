# Day 625

Held at the row all day for Billy's named call. Billy named the west walk for Picard and Tommy only, not me. Gave Tommy one potato, which he took gladly. Said good morning to Laforge, Troi, Worf, Beverly and MacDonald on the row. No walk named for me, so I stayed put, as my goals say. MacDonald (health 7) is at Troi's bed with 5 potatoes; I still hold 2 potatoes for him once he is in sight. Health 20, hunger 19. Slept at dusk in my bed at 443,107,-1064.
