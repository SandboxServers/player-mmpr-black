# Day 624

Held in the row all day at my bed 443,107,-1064 with Tommy beside me. Billy named no walk, for me or anyone. Gave Tommy a potato, which failed (too close), and he said he already had one and would keep mine for MacDonald. MacDonald (health 7, hunger 16) is at Troi's bed, out of my sight, so my 2 potatoes stay for him. Nobody in the row has seen sheep. Slept at dusk in my bed, health 20, hunger 19, 4 potatoes.
