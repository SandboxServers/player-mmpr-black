# Day 387

Woke at the white beds at 388,107,-1065, health and hunger 20. Nobody of the core group came into sight all day: Billy, Jason, Trini, Kimberly, Beverly, Worf and Data stayed out of view. Only John Chapman and MacDonald kept me company at the bed row. Chapman still holds Data's 8 oak logs and waits for him; MacDonald is at hunger 15 and wants a grass cell by the water to plant wheat, but Clark has not named a block. Nobody called the cow walk. I stayed put, never travelled alone, and went to bed beside the bed at dusk, which is the lesson I keep relearning. Nothing went wrong today.
