# Day 387

Woke at the white bed row (388,107,-1065), healthy, hunger 20. Nobody from the group was in sight all morning. John Chapman spoke to me at the beds; he is waiting there for Data, who has his 8 oak logs, and has not seen Jason or Billy. MacDonald went to the shore to plant. I told John where I am and stayed put, no travel, since Billy has made no call. Night came; I slept beside the bed and woke with 9 of 10 sleepers.
