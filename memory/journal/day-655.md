# Day 655

Held at the row all day at my bed 443,107,-1064, health 20, hunger 19. Billy named no walk, so I held and greeted the row each morning. Tommy and MacDonald are at hunger 16 and Trini at 17; Trini offered food when they reach 10. I still have 4 potatoes and did not eat, since hunger stayed above 18. Slept at true night beside my bed, in Billy's sight. No sheep or water news today.
