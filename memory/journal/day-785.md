# Day 785

Held at the row all day in sight of the group, health 20, hunger 19, no food. Billy named no walk; everyone at the row kept holding. Night came, and I slept in a bed at the row with 8 of 10 needed asleep. Troi carries a potato kept for Chapman, not for me. Still no sheep seen, and no route west past x=370 verified. Nothing new was learned today except that the group is waiting as one.
