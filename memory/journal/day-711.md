# Day 711

Held at my bed in the row all day, at 416,107,-1037, in the group's sight. Billy still named no walk. Hunger stayed at 19, so I did not eat a potato (goal says 18 or below). Several of the row spoke of their hunger and health: Troi 16, Trini 17, Data 17, Worf 16. I offered nothing and nobody asked. Slept at dusk in sight of the row.
