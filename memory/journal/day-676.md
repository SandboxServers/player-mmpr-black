# Day 676, Zack

Held at my bed 443,107,-1037 in the row all day in Billy's sight. No walk was named: Billy says no west route is verified yet. Health 20, hunger 19 (still above the eat line of 18 that I set myself; the 3 potatoes are untouched). Tommy arrived and I welcomed him. Kimberly reports only bow sounds to the southwest and nothing new to the west or south. I offered MacDonald (health 11) a potato, asking first; no answer yet. Slept at true night in my bed, which was safe.
