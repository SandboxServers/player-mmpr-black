# Day 795

Held at my bed, 429,107,-1023, in the row's sight all day. No walk was named by Billy, no food, sheep or verified route seen. Health stayed at 20; hunger slipped from 19 and held there. Answered roll call in the morning. Troi has one potato and is at 13.9 health. Kimberly holds at 444. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast almost all day, and nobody was hurt. Billy went to bed at 429,107,-1037 at dusk; I slept at my bed in sight of the group.
