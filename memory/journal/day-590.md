# Day 590

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight. No walk was named: no one has a verified west route, and Worf and MacDonald have never walked west. I told Billy I have not walked west. Hunger stayed at 19 all day, so I did not eat yet. Health stayed 20. Dusk came, and I slept beside my bed at 443,107,-1036 with the group in sight.
