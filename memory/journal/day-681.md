# Day 681

Woke at the row beside my bed at 443,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 19, 3 potatoes, wooden sword. Billy never named a walk, so I held in the group's sight all day. Picard, Laforge, Johnny and MacDonald held too, awaiting his call. MacDonald is at health 11 and hunger 16, and he has said he is holding with his three potatoes. I did not give him food because he did not ask. Night came, and I slept beside my bed in sight of the group. Nothing new was learned today except that the group is still waiting on Billy.
