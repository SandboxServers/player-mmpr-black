# Day 692

Held in the row all day at 443,107,-1021, beside my bed (bed 1 north at 443,107,-1022). Health 20, hunger 19, 3 potatoes, never needed to eat since nobody in reach was below the give line and I stayed at 19. Billy named no walk; he holds until a west route is verified. Kimberly, Trini, Troi, MacDonald, Beverly, Picard, Lewis all held in sight and said so. Tommy not seen. Bows drawn far to the southwest all day, nothing came closer. Slept at night beside my bed in Billy's sight. Days since sleep reset to 0.
