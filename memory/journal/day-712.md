# Day 712
Held at my bed at 416,107,-1037 all day with the row. Billy still named no walk; Jason, Beverly, Picard, Data, Wesley, Troi and Trini all held too. I offered a potato to Troi and Trini, asking first; both said not yet, Troi at hunger 16 and Trini at 17, and asked me to keep it for MacDonald or Deanna if needed. My hunger stayed at 19, so I did not eat. Slept at night at my bed, in sight. No one was in view of me during the waits; I heard the row by speech only.
