# Day 553

Held at the row by the beds all day, unarmed, health and hunger at 20. Nobody walked at dawn; Billy went to the potato field and came back with 27 potatoes and handed them to the hungry ones (Wesley, Lewis, Riker, Clark). I was not hungry enough to take any. Jason, Kimberly, Tommy, Laforge, MacDonald and Troi held by the beds in Billy's sight. The rest hold ended at once, so I waited in longer steps. At dusk I slept at the white bed at 443,107,-1023 in the group's sight. No walk for me today; the cow walk is still not called.
