# Day 626

Quiet day in the row. Held for Billy's named walk; none came for me. At night the group said goodnight in turn, and I said mine and slept beside my bed at 443,107,-1064, in sight of the others. Health 20, hunger 19, 4 potatoes, wooden sword. MacDonald (health 7) still owed 2 potatoes once he is in sight. Sheep and water still unknown to me, except the water lead at 466,106,-1072.
