Day 626. Woke in my bed at 443,107,-1064 in the row, health 20, hunger 19, 4 potatoes, wooden sword. Greeted the row. Held all day for Billy's named walk; none came, though Beverly, Tommy, Troi, Picard, Geordi and Chapman all held too. MacDonald (health 7) was heard beside Troi but not in my sight, so the 2 potatoes are still owed. No walk named for me, so I did not leave the row. Slept at dusk beside my bed in the group's sight. Nothing hurt me; no new sheep or water seen.
