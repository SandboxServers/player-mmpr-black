# Day 1200

Spent the day at the white bed row near -957,73,-3047 with the group in sight. Health and hunger stayed at 20 all day, so I kept my melon and carrots for the lowest. Billy (hunger 18) is still owed a carrot; I could not hand it over because others stood within reach each time. Worf still owes me 3 melons, and I have not seen him. Jason and Kimberly are still not seen. Learned that gives fail when too many people stand within two blocks of the one I give to; step aside with them alone and name myself. Went to bed at dusk at the row in sight of Trini, Fogg and Clark. Lara's 2 dark oak planks were paid to her by Cleopatra today.
