# Day 390

Held at the white beds all day at 388,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20. Nobody from the group came into sight. Overheard MacDonald heading east to the water field to plant. Chapman came to the beds, still holding 8 oak logs for Data; he has not seen Data or Billy. I stayed put, as planned, waiting for Billy's call, and did not travel alone. Slept beside the bed at dusk.
