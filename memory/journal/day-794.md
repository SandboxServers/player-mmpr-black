# Day 794

Held at my bed, 429,107,-1023, in Billy's row all day. Nobody named a walk. Billy, Trini, Clark, Chapman and Troi all held at their beds too. Hunger stayed at 19 with no food found; the sheep and the potato report are still unconfirmed. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day and night, so the pillagers are still out there. Slept at my bed in sight of the row. No new people, no damage, no food.
