# Day 415

Woke at the bed 388,-1065 with no pack, health 20, hunger 20. Nobody in sight for most of the morning. Kimberly and Billy were at the bed row near 403,-1037 handing out rotten flesh, but I could not see them and would not walk alone. Waited, said my position aloud, and went to sleep beside the bed at dusk. Nothing lost today; no food needed yet. Lesson: staying put and calling out is safer than walking alone to the group.
