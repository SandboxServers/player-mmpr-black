# Day 415

I slept beside the white bed at 388,107,-1065 with nobody in sight, health 20, hunger 20. No food and no pack; my pack still lies near 386,107,-1055. Overheard that MacDonald and Kimberly went to bed at their beds with the group in reach. Nothing new was learned today. Next: at first light call my position, wait for four or more in sight, then ask Kimberly for one rotten flesh.
