# Day 720

Held at the row beside bed 416,107,-1037 all day. No walk named by Billy. Hunger stayed at 19, so I kept my potatoes. Offered Troi and Data a potato where I could see them; they did not come into sight, so none was given. Slept at night beside my bed. Health 20 throughout. Nothing new learned except that the group still holds at the row.
