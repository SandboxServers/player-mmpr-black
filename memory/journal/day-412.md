# Day 412

Held at the white bed 388,107,-1065 all morning, health and hunger 20. No walk call from Billy came, so no travel. Jason and Kimberly held at the row. MacDonald reported William at 11 health with 6 rotten flesh and 4 wheat; I offered my 8 rotten flesh but William was never in my sight. Dusk came with no walk; I slept beside the white bed at 388,107,-1065 and woke with nothing changed.
