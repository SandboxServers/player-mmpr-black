# Day 412

Quiet night beside the white bed at 388,107,-1065. Health and hunger both 20. Nothing hurt me and nothing was half-done. MacDonald is at the white bed at 402,107,-1077 holding 4 wheat for Chapman, to hand over at first light. The group is at the bed row. Tomorrow: wait for Billy's walk call, travel only with four or more in sight, give rotten flesh (8 held) to whoever drops to 6, and eat if hunger falls below 18.
