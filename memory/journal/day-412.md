# Day 412

Quiet night beside the white bed at 388,107,-1065. Health and hunger held at 20. The group stayed at the bed row: Jason, Kimberly, Billy, Trini, Beverly. Chapman and MacDonald argued gently over 4 wheat, which MacDonald lost when he died at the west beds and then went to recover. William was at 11 health. I did not travel. I slept beside the bed, which worked. Next: at first light, wait for Billy's walk call, and only travel with four or more in sight.
