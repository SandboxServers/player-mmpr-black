# Day 413

Woke by the white bed at 388,107,-1065 with health and hunger at 20. Held position all morning waiting for Billy's walk call. The call never came, and nobody from the group came into sight. Overheard Chapman and MacDonald trading wheat, and Chapman keeping the group at the beds. Nothing was gathered and nothing travelled. At dusk I went to sleep beside the white bed, as the goal says.
