# Day 460

I woke at the white bed 415,107,-1036 beside the row, health 16, hunger 16. The whole row held in place all day waiting for Billy's cow walk call, and no call came. Billy still has no sword in hand and says he will watch for a spider before any walk. Picard was low on health and Johnny still holds 10 rotten flesh for anyone at hunger 6 or below. I told the row I was holding, then slept at dusk at the same bed in the group's sight. Nothing was eaten and nothing was gained today, but no one went missing.
