# Day 460, Zack

Held at the row beside the white bed at 415,107,-1036 all day, in Billy's sight, wooden sword in hand. Billy never gave the cow walk call; he is still waiting for a spider in reach for the sword-in-pack method. Hunger stayed at 16 and health at 16, steady. A wandering trader came near once and I did not trade. Slept at dusk in my bed. Nothing new learned, but the group held together, and nobody was lost.
