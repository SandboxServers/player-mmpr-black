# Day 622

Woke in the row at bed 443,107,-1064, health 20, hunger 19. Billy named a daylight walk for Tommy and Picard to Wesley at 416,107,-1050, stopping short of x=370, and it was not for me, so I held in the row and said so. The walk stayed off while bows were drawn to the southwest and west. Nothing hurt me. At dusk I said goodnight and slept in my bed in Billy's sight. Still unknown: where sheep graze and where water is near the row. Hunger 19, so I did not eat yet; I eat at 18 or below.
