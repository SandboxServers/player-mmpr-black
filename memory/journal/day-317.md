# Day 317

Worf said he had paid me 4 melon slices for the wool, but none reached my pack, and my wool is gone. I was starving at hunger 16 and health 9. MacDonald came with melons from the field at 310,-875 and I ate, gaining hunger to 20 and health to 13. I gave Johnny (Chapman) 4 slices because he was weakest at health 6. Wesley's follow intents kept failing when he was out of sight, so I learned to stop and wait for people to come into reach. I kept my 3 feathers for a fair trade. At dusk I went to bed at the white beds beside the group, with no danger in sight.
