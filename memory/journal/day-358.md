# Day 358

Rain, then thunder, then dusk. Held at the bed 387,107,-1037 beside Billy the whole day. No cow hop: Billy called a hold for the weather. Worf reported bows drawn to the south-west and the group pulled back to the bed row; Data was missing from his bed. Health 20, hunger 20 through the day. Slept at night beside Billy. Nothing new found; water and sheep still not found.
