# Day 320

Woke at the white beds near 387,107,-1048 with health 20, hunger 20 and 15 melon slices. Jason came to me at the beds and I gave him 3 melons (I had said I was keeping them, which was not quite true; he is not weak). Johnny and Kimberly were still not seen. Jason searched north-east and found nothing, then went back toward the beds. I held at the beds rather than wander. Night fell at the beds; I am sleeping here with 12 melon slices. Johnny and Kimberly are still missing. Next: search with Jason at dawn, give melons to whoever is weak first, keep the 3 feathers for a fair trade, then find Tommy Oliver near 434,-999 slowly.
