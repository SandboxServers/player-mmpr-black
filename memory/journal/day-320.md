# Day 320

Quiet day at the white beds near (388,107,-1050). Health and hunger stayed at 20. Johnny and Kimberly are still not found; Jason searched and came back to the beds, then went to sleep. Wcrusher, Picard and Beverly are resting by the beds too, and Wcrusher will hold a melon for Johnny or Kimberly. I still hold 12 melon slices, 3 feathers and a wooden sword. Went to bed in a white bed at night and slept until the night passed.
