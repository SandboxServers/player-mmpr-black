# Day 671

Thunder by day, then dusk. No walk was named by Billy all day, so I held at my bed in the row in his sight and said so once. Health stayed 20; hunger stayed 19, so I did not eat yet. Nobody came to harm. Lewis stood beside me at dusk and went to his bed. Slept at my bed near 443,107,-1037 with the row around me. Nothing new was learned today except that the row is still holding.
