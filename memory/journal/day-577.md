# Day 577, Zack

Stayed with the group all day. Held at the potato field through the afternoon of 576 and the night, with phantoms attacking; killed one with the wooden sword and ate my last potato at hunger 17. Billy named the walk back to the row, and Tommy and I came in sight; Tommy gave me a potato. At the row I held beside the white bed with Kimberly in sight through dusk, then slept at full night beside the bed at 429,107,-1050, in group sight. Ended the day at health 17, hunger 17. Learned: a use on a ripe potato block does not harvest it; ask for food from someone who holds it. Billy keeps the rule: walk only when named, in company.
