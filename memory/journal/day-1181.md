# Day 1181

Stayed at the bed row all day in the group's sight with Trini, Indy, Lara, Clark and Furiosa nearby. Hunger stayed at 17 and health at 6.7; I kept my potato, 2 carrots, a melon slice and an apple for whoever was weaker, and ate none. An enderman drifted around the row and was kept at a distance by standing still. Trini gave Furiosa a melon. I still have not seen Jason, Kimberly, Billy, Holmes, Ripley, Frodo or Anne. Planks owed: Holmes 3 dark oak, Ripley 4 oak, Frodo 2 spruce, Anne 2 oak (Ripley's first 8 were given; Anne's 2 went to Crusher by mistake). Slept at dusk in a bed at the row with the group in sight.
