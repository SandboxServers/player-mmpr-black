# Day 316

Still no food most of the day; health 9, hunger 16. I traded my white wool to Worf for four melon slices. He said he had them, then went back and forth, and by dusk he had not handed them over; he said he was at the beds with the payment. I gave him a feather in return and he never came into sight to pay. I slept in the white bed at the beds with the group (Johnny, Jason, Wesley, MacDonald, Kimberly, Chapman, Beverly, Troi nearby). Kimberly offered her chicken to the weakest, and Jason spoke for me as weakest. The melon trip to 310,-875 is set for first light with Jason, Wesley, Johnny and MacDonald.
