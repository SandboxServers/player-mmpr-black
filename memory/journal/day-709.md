# Day 709
Held at the row all day at bed 416,107,-1037, in sight of the group. No walk named by Billy yet. Health 20, hunger 19, still holding 3 potatoes; I did not eat because hunger was above 18. Troi (hunger 16) said she would ask me for food when she reaches 10; I offered my potatoes in the row. Worf reported cows at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113; no sheep seen. Slept at dusk beside my bed in the group's sight.
