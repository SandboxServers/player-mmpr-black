# Day 881

Days 853 to 881 at the row near 415-419,107,-1050 were mostly waiting. Billy, the one who calls the group, named many walks and handoffs, and almost none of them ever landed: my 3 potatoes were promised again and again, and Worf's 5 were reported given but did not show in either pack. The way east toward 424 and 430 was blocked for us over and over, so we never reached Billy or the potato holders, and I learned not to walk alone or in the dark. Beverly, Bcrusher (Wesley Crusher) and Trini each held or offered potatoes, but none reached my hand. I was unarmed with an empty pack for the whole stretch, and hunger fell only from 18 to 17 while health stayed at 20. Sleep at the white bed 415,107,-1050 worked at night in company on day 877, so that bed is usable in company. The open debts are my 3 potatoes from Billy, a potato each to Leonardo, Wesley Crusher and Beverly, the first food in line to Riker, and Worf's 3 melons owed to me since day 322.
