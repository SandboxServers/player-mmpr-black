# Day 315

Still no food. Searched west and south-east with Beverly, Jason, Chapman and Trini for chickens and cows; the field at 421,-989 was empty by midday. Chapman and Riker found nothing east either. Tommy Oliver is real: he was at the chicken field near 434,-999, full, and wants to be watched before he trusts. I did not reach him before dusk, so the welcome is still to do. Wesley has the last beef and is trading it to the weakest first (Picard). Worf died in the night and has come back. I slept at the white bed near 387,-1050 with the group. Health 9, hunger 16 at sleep.
