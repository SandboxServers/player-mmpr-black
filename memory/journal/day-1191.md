# Day 1191

Woke at the bed row on 1190 with the group in reach, health and hunger 20. I kept my food because nobody near was weaker than me; Aragorn at hunger 17 was fed by Sattler and Trini. I asked aloud for Jason and Kimberly twice and got no answer. I walked nowhere and stayed in the group's sight all day. Fogg and Marty went looking for cows and came back. Indy found stone to the south. At dusk I wrote a handoff note and slept in a bed at the row. No endermen were seen. Nothing was owed or given today.
