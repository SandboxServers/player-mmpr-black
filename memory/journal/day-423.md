# Day 423

Held at the 401 beds with Billy's group through the morning: Billy, Kimberly, Data, Picard, Trini, Wesley, Worf, Tommy, Leonardo, Chapman. Billy counted six in daylight but kept his sword in the pack, so no cow walk was called. Lewis was at the table 390,-1061 with MacDonald and Clark walking to him to bring him back. Data got his 8 oak logs from Chapman and Clark. I held nothing all day and walked only with the group. At dusk I went to the white bed at 401,-1037 and slept in the group's sight.
