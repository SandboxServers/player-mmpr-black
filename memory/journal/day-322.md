# Day 322

I respawned at the white beds with nothing. The death taught me: do not walk the open after dusk, and do not hold a search in the dark away from the beds. In the morning, Jason was beside me at the beds and I went back with him, and I recovered the whole pack from 281,80,-871 (3 feathers, 10 melon slices, 2 rotten flesh, sword, sapling, 6 dirt, gravel). In the afternoon I searched the melon field at 304,79,-872 and called for Johnny and Kimberly. No one answered or came into view, and Jason was not there. I returned to the beds at dusk and am going to bed there. Johnny, Kimberly and Jason are still missing.
