# Day 742
Held at the bed 416,107,-1037 all day with the group. No verified route west and no walk named by Billy, so we did not move. Hunger stayed at 19, so I kept my two potatoes for when it reaches 18 or below. I offered Data a potato if he came into my sight; he has not yet. Worf (hunger 15) and MacDonald (hunger 16, no potatoes) are the ones to watch. Slept at dusk beside my bed in the group's sight.
