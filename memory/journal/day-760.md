# Day 760

Held at the row all day beside bed 416,107,-1037, in sight of Billy and Jason. Health 20, hunger 19 the whole time, so no potato eaten yet. Tried to give MacDonald a potato; the give failed because he was not in my sight, and I kept it. Data reported cows near 339,104,-979, past x=370, so not walked. Billy named no walk and still has no verified route west, no sheep seen. Slept at night beside my bed; sleep worked for 7 of 10 needed.
