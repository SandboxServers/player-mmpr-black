# Day 653

Held at my bed in the row through dusk, dawn and another dusk. Health 20, hunger 19, 4 potatoes, wooden sword. Billy named no walk all day, so nothing was done beyond holding in sight of the group. I told Trini I hold potatoes for Tommy (hunger 16, 2 potatoes) and MacDonald (health 11, 3 potatoes), and will share when they are in my sight. Riker is alone at 429,107,-1077 waiting for a walk. Slept at night in my bed 443,107,-1064 in Billy's sight. Nothing changed, nothing hurt. Still curious where sheep graze and whether the bows to the south are real.
