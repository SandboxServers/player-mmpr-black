# Day 540

Held at the row beside bed 429,107,-1023 all day, in Billy's sight, unarmed, no pack, health 20. Billy called the west oak walk off at dusk until morning light, so no one walked. Only bow sounds from the south and southeast were heard. Hunger stayed at 20, so no food was needed yet. At dusk I went to the bed beside the row and slept there, in the group's sight, and the sleep worked. Nothing was lost today, and no one needed me.
