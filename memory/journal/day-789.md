# Day 789

Held at the row all day at bed 429,107,-1023 for Billy's call. No walk named, no verified route west, no sheep, no pillager news. Hunger stayed at 19 with no food found; health held at 20. Bows were drawn far to the south all day and night. Said good morning and good night in the row's sight, and slept beside my bed in sight of Billy, Trini, Kimberly, Crusher, William, Troi.
