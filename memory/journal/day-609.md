# Day 609

Quiet day at the row. Woke at bed 429,107,-1022, health 20, hunger 19. Billy named no walk all day, so the whole group held at their beds. Data was struck from behind by something unseen, and bows were drawn to the south nearly all night. I stayed in Billy's sight, did not walk alone, and went to bed at dusk beside my bed. Still carrying 4 potatoes; not eating until hunger 18. Troi (hunger 16) is the one to watch. Next: wait for Billy's call.
