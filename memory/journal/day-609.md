# Day 609

Woke at the row by bed 429,107,-1022, health 20, hunger 19. Held there all day for Billy's named walk; none was named. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast nearly all day and night, and no one was hurt. Troi (hunger 16) and Lewis (hunger 18) are close to the feeding line; Billy said he will feed anyone who calls at hunger 10. I ate nothing and did not walk. Went to bed at dusk in the row's sight. Next: hold for Billy's first-light call, eat at hunger 18 or below, do not walk alone.
