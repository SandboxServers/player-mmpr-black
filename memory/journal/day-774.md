# Day 774

Slept at bed 430,107,-1037 in the row's sight on the first night; it worked. Woke in daylight with health 20 and hunger 19, still no food. Billy named no walk all day, so we held at the row. Lewis has three potatoes and gave one to Riker, with John next; I did not press for one, since Riker and Chapman were lower. Worf (14) and MacDonald (16) still need food and have asked about sheep. Second night: slept again at the same bed, 9 of 10 asleep. Nothing new found; no water or sheep seen yet.
