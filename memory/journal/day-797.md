# Day 797, Zack

Woke at my bed 429,107,-1023 with the row. Health 20, hunger 19, wooden sword only. Billy, Clark, Beverly, Kimberly, John and Troi all held at the row with no walk, no verified route, no sheep and no food. Bows were drawn to the south and southeast all day, never close. I said roll call and held for Billy's call. Nothing new was found. Went to sleep at my bed in Billy's sight at nightfall.
