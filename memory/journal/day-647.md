# Day 647

Held at the row all day with the group, beside my bed 443,107,-1064. Billy named no walk, so nobody left. Health 20, hunger 19, 4 potatoes, still above the eating line of 18. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16, 2 potatoes) and MacDonald (health 7) are still the weakest; neither was within sight to share with. Slept at night in my bed, in the row's sight. No new food, sheep or water found. Next: hold for Billy's call, tell him first.
