# Day 393

Quiet day at the white bed at 388,107,-1065. Group never came into sight; Chapman and MacDonald held and talked at the beds, and MacDonald went to the shore to plant. No one asked for food, so the 8 rotten flesh stayed with me. Health and hunger held at 20. Waited for Billy's call through the day and it did not come. Dusk came at the bed row and I stayed beside the bed.
