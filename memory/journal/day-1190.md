# Day 1190

Woke at the bed row with health and hunger at 20. Billy is alive and in sight there, and he has seen no Jason or Kimberly and no cows. Trini and Tommy have not seen them either. Fogg, Marty and Indy went south-west for stone and food; Fogg came back empty. I kept to the row with the group in sight all day, held my food, and went to bed at dusk with no endermen in sight. The day brought no news of the missing friends, so the search goes on in company.
