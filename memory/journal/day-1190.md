# Day 1190

Night at the white bed row near -952,73,-3047, with Billy, Trini, Tommy, Magellan, Bilbo and others in sight. Health and hunger stayed at 20 all day. Jason and Kimberly are still unseen; I asked the group aloud and got no sign of them. Billy asked for melon; Trini will hand one over when Billy stands clear. I told Billy I will give food only when he is alone with me and hungrier than I am. I slept in a bed at the row. Carrying 1 melon slice, 2 carrots, 1 baked potato. Owed planks and potatoes remain as in the summary; none given today.
