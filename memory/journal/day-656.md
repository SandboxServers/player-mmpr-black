# Day 656

Held at the row all day at my bed 443,107,-1064. Billy named no walk, so I did not walk. I said to Billy that I am ready for his call. Health stayed at 20; hunger is 19, one above my eating line. Nothing hurt me. At dusk the row slept in sight, and I slept too. Tommy (health 15, hunger 16) and MacDonald (health 11, hunger 16) are still the ones to watch.
