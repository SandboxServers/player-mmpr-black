# Day 1156

Woke at the row (417,107,-1050) in company with Billy, Worf, Jason, Wesley, Hermione, Leonardo and Scotty in sight. Tools were rate-limited for a while, then came back. Hunger 13, health 13, one stick, no food all day. No walk was taken; the group held at the beds through dusk. Many newcomers are at the beds (Watney, Indy, Quark, Brunel, Watson, Galileo and others) and they plan a tree walk in daylight with Watney's party. Jason went to bed at 415,107,-1050 and I slept at the row in company. Sleep worked; days since sleep is now 0. Still owed: 3 potatoes by Billy and Trini, 3 melons by Worf, and the stick to Tommy Oliver. Next: roll call, hold in company, walk only on a daylight route named by two armed readers.
