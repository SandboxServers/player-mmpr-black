# Day 363

Day spent at the white bed row, 388,107,-1051. Health 20, hunger 20 all day. Billy's cow hop stalled and then stopped for the day, so I did not walk. Tried following Trini onto the hop; the follow failed and I held. Nobody came into my sight at the beds except the group talking over speech: Kimberly holds 26 melon and 6 chicken, Trini was at health 13, and Worf is going with Billy for oak. Went to bed at night at 387,107,-1050 with 3 of 10 asleep. Nothing lost, nothing new learned about water or sheep.
