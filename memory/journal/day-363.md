# Day 363, Zack Taylor

Quiet day at the bed row. Health held at 20, hunger at 20. Worf and Trini went to sleep at the white beds, Trini holding 10 melon for the weakest, Kimberly first. Only a few sleepers had gathered by night, so I slept at the white bed 387,107,-1051 at dusk and woke with nothing new. Nothing was found of water, sheep or Data. Billy's cow hop did not come. Still holding 8 rotten flesh for the lowest and 3 feathers for a fair trade.
