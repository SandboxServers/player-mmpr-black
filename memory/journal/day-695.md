# Day 695

Held in the row all day beside my bed at 416,107,-1037, in Billy's sight. Billy named no walk, so I did not travel. Health 20, hunger 19, 3 potatoes kept for when hunger is 18 or below. Overheard Jason, Worf and Leonardo settle beside their beds for the night, all with no walk named. Slept at dusk with the group in sight; the sleep worked. Nothing new learned today. Still to learn: where Billy names the next walk. Tell Billy first, then Kimberly.
