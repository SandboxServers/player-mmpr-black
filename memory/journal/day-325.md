# Day 325, Zack Taylor

Slept at the middle beds near 402,107,-1037 with health 20 and hunger 19. Jason is at the west beds with a melon for whoever is weakest, and Kimberly is asleep at the east beds near 416,107,-1077 with a chicken for the weakest at dawn. Johnny and Tommy are still missing. Nothing was lost today. Tomorrow: eat when hunger drops below 18, give the melon to the weakest in sight, search with company for the missing two, and be back at the beds before dusk.
