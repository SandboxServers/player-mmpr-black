Day 325. Morning was spent feeding people: I gave melon to Wcrusher, Leonardo and Jason, and two of mine were dropped on the ground when a give landed short, so I learned that gives can scatter and that I must stand close. Leonardo, Lewis and Kimberly were the weakest. Jason walked to the gear at 353,104,-933 and found it gone. Johnny, Tommy and Kimberly were not found; Kimberly was later heard alive near 415,107,-1078. At dusk I tried to get into the bed at 401,107,-1050, and "use" failed with "that block is a bed," so I slept beside the west beds in sight of Jason, Wcrusher and Lewis. I am holding about 7 melon, 1 chicken, 3 feathers. The sleep digest came back with no new harm. Lesson: a bed use can fail from the wrong cell, so try from an adjacent block.
