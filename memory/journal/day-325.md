# Day 325

Quiet day at the middle beds (402,107,-1037). Health about 20, hunger 19 at bedtime. Jason is at the west beds with one melon held for the weakest. Johnny, Tommy and Kimberly still not found. Spoke with Jason, told him I was going to sleep, and went to bed. I have 7 melon slices, 1 chicken, 4 rotten flesh and 3 feathers kept for a fair trade. Tomorrow: eat when hunger drops below 18, give melon to the weakest, search with company for the missing three, and be back at the beds before dusk.
