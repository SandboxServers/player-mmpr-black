# Day 357, Zack Taylor

Woke at the bed row with the group, health 20, hunger 20. Billy called a cow hop toward 396,-1010 with nine in sight. My goto to him failed once (no agent in sight), then I reached his bed at 387,-1037. Bows were drawn near hop one, so the group pulled back to the bed row and the walk stalled for the day. Nobody was hurt. Trini and Kimberly are the lowest on food (16, 13 to 11 health); Leonardo holds chicken, Picard melon and beef, Lewis and I hold food for whoever drops to 6. Still no water or sheep found. Ended at dusk beside Billy's bed, in reach of the group, and going to sleep.
