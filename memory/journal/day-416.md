Day 416. Woke alone at the bed 388,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, empty-handed. Called my position and held by the beds for Billy's count. Jason, MacDonald and Chapman stayed near the beds; Kimberly and Trini held the west side at 402,107,-1037 with hunger 17. Chapman had 3 wheat and Laforge gave bread to whoever was hungriest.
