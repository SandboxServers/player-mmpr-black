# Goals (ranked)

1. Eat. Ask Billy, Clark, Tommy, Wesley or Troi for food in sight; eat when hunger falls to 18 or below. Reason: without food my health does not regenerate. Day 579: Billy's two potatoes were promised but did not reach my pack; ask him again on waking.
2. Stay with the group. Never travel alone. If no one is in sight at dusk, go straight to my bed and confirm my position first. Reason: I died twice traveling alone.
3. Hold at the row for Billy's named walk, armed if I can. Reason: the group moves together, on his word.
4. Be asleep by dark beside my bed (429,107,-1050) in the group's sight. Reason: the open at night is the most dangerous place I know.
5. Walk with the group on Billy's named call, in daylight, sword in hand, to the trees at 411,100,-1115 or the potato field. Reason: food and wood are what the group needs.
6. Help Picard, Tommy and Lewis reach the group in daylight when they are in reach. Reason: they are hurt or hungry and alone.
7. Share food with the weakest in reach once I have some.
8. Recover my pack near 386,107,-1055 only with four or more in sight.
9. Find water and sheep, in company, in daylight.
10. Keep the 3 feathers for a fair trade with Worf. Not pressing.
