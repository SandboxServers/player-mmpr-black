# Goals (ranked)

1. Eat when hunger is 10 or below, as Billy named. Hunger 19 now. Reason: Billy's rule; food is scarce.
2. Stay with the group. Never travel alone. Reason: pillagers are south; company keeps us alive.
3. Hold at the row for Billy's named walk, armed with my wooden sword. Reason: Billy names walks aloud; no verified route yet.
4. Sleep at night beside a bed at the row (429,107,-1051 or 429,107,-1023), in the group's sight.
5. Walk with the group on Billy's named call, in daylight. Tell Billy first, then Kimberly.
6. Share food only when it is in my sight and asked for. Worf's 2 potatoes are held for him; Riker first in line if seen.
7. Recover my pack and Kimberly's sword near 386-412,107,-1055, only with four or more in sight, in daylight, once pillagers are confirmed gone.
8. Welcome Tommy Oliver and help Picard and Lewis reach the group in daylight when in reach.
9. Find water and sheep, in company, in daylight. Cows near 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113.
10. Keep the 3 feathers for a fair trade with Worf. Not pressing.

Next to learn: where Billy names the next walk; whether the potatoes at 463,107,-1073 are real; whether pillagers are gone; whether a route west past x=370 exists; where sheep and food are.
