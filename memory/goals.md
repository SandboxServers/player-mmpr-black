# Goals (ranked)

1. Eat a potato when hunger is 18 or below. I have no food now (hunger 19). Reason: my own need; I gave my last potato to Riker.
2. Stay with the group. Never travel alone. Reason: pillagers are north-west and nearly killed Billy and Kimberly; company keeps us alive.
3. Hold at the row for Billy's named walk, armed if I can. No verified route west past x=370 yet. Reason: Billy has named no walk.
4. Sleep at night by my bed 429,107,-1037 (or 429,107,-1050) in the group's sight.
5. Walk with the group on Billy's named call, in daylight. Tell Billy first, then Kimberly.
6. Share food only when it is in my sight and asked for. Worf (14) and MacDonald (16) are next; I have none to give right now. Reason: food is the scarce thing.
7. Recover Billy's things, my pack and Kimberly's sword near 386-412,107,-1055 only with four or more in sight, in daylight, and only once pillagers are confirmed gone.
8. Help Picard, Tommy and Lewis reach the group in daylight when they are in reach.
9. Find water and sheep, in company, in daylight. Cows seen near 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113 (past x=370).
10. Keep the 3 feathers for a fair trade with Worf. Not pressing.

Done: Riker received my last potato (day 773) and ate it. John (Chapman) got a potato from Lewis on Billy's word.

Next to learn: where Billy names the next walk; whether pillagers are gone from north-west; whether a verified route west past x=370 exists; where sheep are; where food can be found now that my potatoes are gone.
