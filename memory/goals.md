# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay fed and share. Eat if hunger falls below 18; give food to the weakest in reach. I hold 8 rotten flesh for whoever drops to 6. Watch Kimberly (health 11) and Trini (hunger 16).
2. Stay with the group. Never travel until the group is in sight. Never travel alone.
3. Be at the beds before dusk, asleep by dark.
4. Walk to the cows at 364,-1010 and chickens at 360,-1008 only when Billy calls and the group is in sight.
5. Find water and sheep, in company, in daylight. Clark is searching west.
6. Check on Kimberly and Troi when seen, in company.
7. Keep the 3 feathers for a fair trade. Worf owes me 3 melon; not pressing.
