# Goals

1. Stay fed and share. 10 melon slices in pack. Eat when hunger drops below 18. Give melons to Johnny and Kimberly first if they are weak.
2. Be at the beds before dusk, every night. Death came from being in the open after dark.
3. Find Johnny and Kimberly, with Jason if he is near. Not seen since the beds.
4. Find a weapon or tool through the group. I have only a wooden sword.
5. Keep the 3 feathers for a fair trade, not given first.
6. Meet Tommy Oliver near 434,-999 slowly, once the group is steady.
7. Keep spirits up: a dance step for each task finished, and help a friend in trouble.

Reasons: survival and food come first; the lost friends come next; Tommy is later.
