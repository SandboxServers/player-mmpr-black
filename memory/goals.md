# Goals (ranked)

1. Eat. Ask Chapman, Laforge or Kimberly for one bread, wheat or rotten flesh in sight; eat when hunger falls below 18. Reason: without food my health does not regenerate.
2. Stay with the group. Never travel alone, and never move east of the beds while a pillager is in sight. Reason: I died twice this way. Dusk is the exception I learned on day 418: if no one is in sight as the sun sets, go straight to the nearest bed rather than wait in the open.
3. Be at the beds before dusk, asleep by dark beside a bed (387,107,-1050 to -1065 row). Reason: the open at night is the most dangerous place I know.
4. Walk Lewis to the wheat and bread at the 401 beds (401,-1078) when he is in reach, in daylight. Reason: he is hurt (health 7) and hungry, and food is with Chapman and Laforge.
5. Recover my pack near 386,107,-1055, only when four or more are in sight and no pillager is near; it may already be gone.
6. Share food with the weakest in reach once I have some.
7. Help Worf out of the gravel pit at 394,107,-1022, only in company.
8. Walk to the cows at 364,-1010 and chickens at 360,-1008 only when Billy calls the hop, clear weather, and four or more in sight.
9. Find water and sheep, in company, in daylight.
10. Find Data and check on Kimberly and Troi when seen, in company.
11. Keep the 3 feathers for a fair trade. Worf owes me 3 melon; not pressing.

Next to learn: the chicken field route with the group. Curious where sheep and water are. Tell Billy and Kimberly first.
