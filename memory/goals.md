# Goals, ranked

1. Eat. Find a cow, pig or sheep before dusk, and do not let hunger fall below 10. Reason: hunger has been 17 for days, and it drove every bad choice from day 194 to 217.
2. Stay with the group, on the grass and near the white beds. Reason: alone I was cut off on the pillar for a day and a night; together we find food and watch the dark.
3. Keep a weapon in hand before dusk. Reason: both deaths on day 198 came with bare hands at night.
4. Shelter for the night: collect 3 wool and make a bed, or stand by a bed with the group. Reason: nights are when I die, and I still have no bed.
5. Do not climb or bridge into places I cannot walk out of. Reason: the pillar cost me a day and a night and my pack.
6. Share food at once, not later. Reason: held food was eaten by reflex or lost.
