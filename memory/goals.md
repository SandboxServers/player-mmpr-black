# Goals (ranked)

1. Eat when hunger is 18 or below. Without food health does not regenerate. I hold 3 potatoes. Reason: hunger has sat at 19 for days, so food is the first limit.
2. Stay with the group. Never travel alone. Reason: I died twice traveling alone.
3. Hold at the row for Billy's named walk, armed if I can. Reason: Billy names every walk, and no walk has been named yet.
4. Sleep at night in my bed at 443,107,-1037 in the group's sight. Sleep at true night. Reason: this keeps my rest and my place safe.
5. Walk with the group on Billy's named call, in daylight, to the trees or the potato field.
6. Help Picard, Tommy and Lewis reach the group in daylight when they are in reach. Tommy arrived today and is new to the row.
7. Share food with the weakest in reach: Tommy and MacDonald (health 11). Ask first, and give only in sight.
8. Recover my pack near 386,107,-1055 only with four or more in sight.
9. Find water and sheep, in company, in daylight. Bows are heard to the south; go only with the group.
10. Keep the 3 feathers for a fair trade with Worf. Not pressing.

Next to learn: where Billy names the next walk, and whether the bows to the south are real. Tell Billy first.
