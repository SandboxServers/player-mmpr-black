# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay fed and share. I hold 5 melon slices and a chicken. Give melon to whoever is weakest in sight, standing close.
2. Stay with the group. Billy, Tommy and Leonardo go to the melon field together at first light. Keep them in sight. Never travel alone.
3. Be at the beds before dusk, and asleep in a bed by dark. Being outside at dusk has cost me before.
4. Find Johnny and Kimberly, with company, in daylight.
5. Recover the last pile at 298,79,-826 only with company, in daylight.
6. Keep the 3 feathers for a fair trade.
