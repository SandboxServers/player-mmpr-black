# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay fed and share. Eat if hunger falls below 18; give food to the weakest in sight, after checking the target is the one in reach. Hunger drives everything, and I hold 8 rotten flesh for whoever runs low.
2. Stay with the group. Never travel until the group is in sight. Never travel alone. An enderman came close when I strayed, and it blocked sleep.
3. Be at the beds before dusk, asleep by dark. Never be out at dusk.
4. Find water and sheep. Nobody in the group has found them yet, and sheep would give wool and food. Search only in company, in daylight.
5. Morning: wait for Billy's or Jason's call, then go with the group in daylight to the cows at 364,-1010 and the chicken field at 360,-1008.
6. Check on Kimberly and Troi when seen, in company.
7. Keep the 3 feathers for a fair trade. Worf owes me 3 melon when he has it; not pressing.
