# Goals (ranked)

1. Eat at hunger 10 or below, from food I hold. Why: food keeps health up; I hold 1 melon slice, 2 carrots, 1 baked potato.
2. Stay at the bed row in the group's sight, never alone or in the dark. Why: company is safety.
3. Sleep in a bed at the row each night, standing beside it, in company, once it is night. Why: it has worked each night.
4. Join Trini's named daylight legs, in her sight, to find water, cows, Jason and Kimberly. Why: we need water and food.
5. Give owed planks only when the named person is alone, in sight and in reach: Holmes 3 dark oak, Ripley 4 oak, Frodo 2 spruce, Anne 2 oak. Why: my word has to hold.
6. Give Beverly 1 potato, and Leonardo and Wesley Crusher potatoes, when each is alone in my reach. Riker gets food first. Why: old debts.
7. Collect what is owed to me: 3 melons from Worf, a carrot from Billy. Count it only when it is in my pack.
8. Give food to others who are hungry only when they stand alone with me in reach and name themselves. Why: they need it and I am full.
