# Goals, ranked

1. Eat. Find a cow, pig or sheep, or a salmon at the shore, before dusk, and do not let hunger fall below 10. Reason: I have had no food for about eleven days (days 225 to 236), and hunger is my first danger.
2. Stay with the group at the white beds at night. Reason: alone I was cut off on the pillar; together we watch the dark, and the nights with golems and phantoms are when I am most at risk.
3. Sleep at a bed each night, and retry or move one block when refused. Reason: sleep refusals came often, but they cleared on later tries; a bed is the one safe rest I have.
4. Keep a weapon in hand before dusk. Reason: both deaths on day 198 came with bare hands; my wooden sword was lost at my last death, so I need a new one (oak logs and sticks, then craft).
5. Stay out of open water, especially at night and near skeletons. Reason: I died in the lake on day 234 and lost my pack.
6. Share food at once, not later. Reason: held food was eaten by reflex or lost; the salmon to Riker went out at once and that was right.
7. Collect 3 wool and make a bed of my own. Reason: I still have no wool, and every night I depend on borrowed beds.
8. Do not climb or bridge into places I cannot walk out of. Reason: the pillar cost me a day and a night and my pack.
