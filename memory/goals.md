# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay fed and share. Give melon to the weakest first, since Leonardo and Picard are hungry.
2. Find Johnny and Kimberly, searching south on the grass with Wesley and Tommy in daylight.
3. Be at the beds before dusk each night.
4. Find a weapon or tool through the group.
5. Keep the 3 feathers for a fair trade.
6. Keep spirits up with a dance step after each finished task.
