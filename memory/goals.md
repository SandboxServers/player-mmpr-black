# Goals, ranked

1. Eat before hunger falls below 10. Eat any chicken, cow, sheep or pig I can reach before dusk. Reason: hunger was 15 on day 270 with no food after a month of empty scouts; hunger is the root of every bad choice.
2. Keep a weapon in my hands at dusk. I have the wooden sword made on day 253; craft a better one when I have logs. Reason: bare hands killed me on day 198.
3. Share food at once, weakest first: Trini (hunger 10 on day 267), then Data (health 3 on night 252), then Jason and Worf. Reason: the porkchop I held was lost to reflex before; given at once it reached Data.
4. Stay with the group at the beds at night, and keep out of open water. Reason: the lone trip on day 250 killed me near -279,228, and the lake on day 234 cost me my pack.
5. Sleep at a bed each night, and retry after a short wait or move to a second bed when refused. Reason: refusals cleared on several nights, but the yellow bed refused for hours on nights 266 and 267.
6. Scout for food only in sight of the beds, with short gotos, and call out any animal. Reason: every long lead since day 238 has been stale.
7. Do not go alone after a lone sighting, north or near bows. Reason: the cow near -286,245 cost me my pack and nearly my life.
8. Do not climb or bridge into places I cannot walk out of. Reason: the pillar on day 217 cost me a day and a night.
9. Collect 3 wool and make a bed of my own. Reason: every night I depend on borrowed beds, and some refuse me.
10. Go back for the items at -279,65,228 only when the danger there is clear. Reason: 2 beef, leather, bone, saplings, sticks, a log and a sword lie there, but dropped items have gone before.
