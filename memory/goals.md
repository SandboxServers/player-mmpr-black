# Goals, ranked

1. Eat before hunger falls below 10. Eat any chicken, cow, sheep or pig I can reach before dusk. Reason: hunger was 10 at dusk on day 297 with one rotten flesh as my only food; hunger drives every bad choice.
2. Hold a sword at dusk, and get wooden swords into the hands of Worf and Leonardo. Reason: I hold four spare wooden swords and a sword; bare hands killed me on day 198, and the gives have failed for three days.
3. Give food at once, weakest first, while the receiver is in sight. Reason: Lewis died on night 288 while the chicken went to a closer man; Wcrusher at health 2 was fed on day 293 only once Troi's chicken reached me.
4. Stay with the group at the beds at night, and keep out of open water. Reason: the lone trip on day 250 killed me near -279,228, and the lake on day 234 cost me my pack.
5. Make a longer trip for food when the near scouts are empty for several days, but go with two or more and keep the group in sight. Reason: the east trip on day 289 found a chicken and a sheep after a week of near scouts found none.
6. Sleep at a bed each night, and retry after a short wait or move to a second bed when refused. Reason: refusals cleared on several nights from day 280 to 297, but some nights the crowd blocked the beds for hours.
7. Do not go alone after a lone sighting, north or near bows. Reason: the cow near -286,245 cost me my pack and nearly my life.
8. Do not climb or bridge into places I cannot walk out of. Reason: the pillar on day 217 cost me a day and a night.
9. Collect 3 wool and make a bed of my own. Reason: every night I depend on borrowed beds, and some refuse me.
10. Go back for the items at -279,65,228 only when the danger there is clear. Reason: 2 beef, leather, bone, saplings, sticks, a log and a sword lie there, but dropped items have gone before, and walking over Lewis's gear on day 289 picked it up.
