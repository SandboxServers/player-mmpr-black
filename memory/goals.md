# Goals (ranked)

1. Eat. Hunger is my first need: I have no food now. Ask Kimberly or Lewis for one rotten flesh or a melon slice, in sight, and eat when hunger falls below 18. Reason: without food my health does not regenerate and a single shot kills me.
2. Stay with the group. Never travel alone, and never move east of the beds while a pillager is in sight. Reason: I died twice this way.
3. Be at the beds before dusk, asleep by dark beside a bed (387,107,-1051 to -1065 row).
4. Recover my pack near 386,107,-1055, only when four or more are in sight and no pillager is near; it may already be gone. Reason: the stone and dirt are useful but not worth another death.
5. Share food with the weakest in reach once I have some. Reason: the group lives by sharing.
6. Help Worf out of the gravel pit at 394,107,-1022, only in company, following Beverly's advice.
7. Walk to the cows at 364,-1010 and chickens at 360,-1008 only when Billy calls the hop, clear weather, and four or more in sight.
8. Find water and sheep, in company, in daylight. Reason: the group needs wool and farm food.
9. Find Data and check on Kimberly and Troi when seen, in company. Chapman's 8 oak logs go to Data.
10. Keep the 3 feathers for a fair trade. Worf owes me 3 melon; not pressing.
