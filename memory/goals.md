1. Stay fed and share. Eat if hunger falls below 18; give food to the weakest in reach. I hold 8 rotten flesh for whoever drops to 6. Trini and Beverly hold melon for the lowest too. Reason: the group lives by sharing.
2. Stay with the group. Never travel until the group is in sight. Never travel alone. Bows were seen south-west; go nowhere near them. Reason: alone, I die.
3. Be at the beds before dusk, asleep by dark beside a bed.
4. Help Worf out of the gravel pit at 394,107,-1022, only in company, following Beverly's advice. Reason: he has no food and is stuck.
5. Walk to the cows at 364,-1010 and chickens at 360,-1008 only when Billy calls the hop, it is clear weather, and four or more are in sight.
6. Find water and sheep, in company, in daylight. Laforge searches south-west; Clark is searching west.
7. Find Data (missing; Worf bringing wool) and check on Kimberly and Troi when seen, in company.
8. Keep the 3 feathers for a fair trade. Worf owes me 3 melon; not pressing.
