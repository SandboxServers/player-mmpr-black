# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay fed and share. Eat if hunger falls below 18; give food to the weakest in reach. I hold 8 rotten flesh for whoever drops to 6. Picard and Beverly hold melon and beef for the lowest too.
2. Stay with the group. Never travel until the group is in sight. Never travel alone. Bows were seen south-west; go nowhere near them.
3. Be at the beds before dusk, asleep by dark.
4. Walk to the cows at 364,-1010 and chickens at 360,-1008 only when Billy calls the hop, it is clear weather, and four or more are in sight.
5. Find water and sheep, in company, in daylight. Laforge searches south-west; Clark is searching west.
6. Find Data (missing; Worf bringing wool) and check on Kimberly and Troi when seen, in company.
7. Keep the 3 feathers for a fair trade. Worf owes me 3 melon; not pressing.
