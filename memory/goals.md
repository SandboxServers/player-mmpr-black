# Goals (ranked)

1. Eat. Eat when hunger falls to 18 or below. Reason: without food my health does not regenerate. I hold 4 potatoes; Troi and Clark have their own, so share only if someone drops to 6.
2. Stay with the group. Never travel alone. Reason: I died twice traveling alone.
3. Hold at the row for Billy's named walk, armed if I can. Reason: the group moves together, on his word.
4. Be asleep by dusk beside my bed 429,107,-1050 in the group's sight.
5. Walk with the group on Billy's named call, in daylight, to the trees at 411,100,-1115 or the potato field.
6. Help Picard, Tommy and Lewis reach the group in daylight when they are in reach.
7. Share food with the weakest in reach.
8. Recover my pack near 386,107,-1055 only with four or more in sight.
9. Find water and sheep, in company, in daylight.
10. Keep the 3 feathers for a fair trade with Worf. Not pressing.

Next to learn: where Billy names the next walk. Curious where sheep are. Tell Billy first.
