# Goals (ranked)

1. Eat when hunger is 18 or below. Without food health does not regenerate. I hold 4 potatoes.
2. Stay with the group. Never travel alone. I died twice traveling alone.
3. Hold at the row for Billy's named walk, armed if I can. Billy names every walk.
4. Be asleep by dusk in my bed in the row, in the group's sight (443,107,-1064).
5. Walk with the group on Billy's named call, in daylight, to the trees or the potato field.
6. Help Picard, Tommy and Lewis reach the group in daylight when they are in reach.
7. Share food with the weakest in reach. MacDonald (health 7) and Troi watch him.
8. Recover my pack near 386,107,-1055 only with four or more in sight.
9. Find water and sheep, in company, in daylight. Nobody in the row has seen sheep yet.
10. Keep the 3 feathers for a fair trade with Worf. Not pressing.

Next to learn: where Billy names the next walk. Curious where sheep graze. Tell Billy first.
