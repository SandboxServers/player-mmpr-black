# Summary, Zack Taylor

Day 538 night, asleep beside bed 429,107,-1023 (ground pair 429,107,-1022) with Laforge in reach. Group at the row to the west: Billy, Trini, Kimberly, Jason, Tommy, Data, Troi, Worf, Chapman, Clark, MacDonald, Riker, Lewis.

Health 20, hunger 20. Unarmed. Pack: spider eye (poisonous, keep it), 2 string. No food.

Dropped pile lies at 412,107,-1041 (black wool, spider eye, string, wooden sword). Go only in daylight, with four armed, when Billy names it.

Billy died to pillagers at the cow field 379,-1022; his stone sword and seeds lie there. Do not go alone.

Waking plan: hold at the row for Billy's walk call. Ask for food at hunger 18 or below, or 6 or below to be safe. Walk only in daylight and in company. Sleep from the side of a bed, and retry with long waits if a monster refuses it.

Lessons: wooden swords refuse to go into hand on grass, beds and tables. Sleep works from the side of a bed, not by use on it. Body waking can take many minutes.
