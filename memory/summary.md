# Summary, Zack Taylor

Day 579 done. Slept at dusk in my bed 429,107,-1050 at the row, in Billy's sight. Woke with health 20, hunger about 19, a wooden sword, no food in pack.

Group at the row: Billy (leader, names every walk and bed), Tommy, Kimberly, Trini, Laforge, Leonardo, Lewis, Clark, Riker, Troi, MacDonald, Picard, Beverly, Wesley, Worf, Jason, Bcrusher, Chapman, William.

Beds named by Billy: mine is 429,107,-1050. Beds refuse use in daylight; sleep at dusk, beside the bed.

Food: ripe potatoes at the field near 467-469,107,-1068 to -1072. Billy promised me two potatoes; they have not reached my pack. Clark has 5 potatoes, Tommy has 4. Eat at hunger 18 or below.

Wood: oak lead at 414,104,-1126. Water lead at 466,106,-1086. Trees north at 411,100,-1115 are the next named walk.

Plan: hold at the row for Billy's named walk. Walk only when named, in daylight, armed, with company.

Lessons: sleep fails at dusk and in daylight without a bed beside. Use on a ripe potato block does not harvest it. Gives fail if the item is on the ground or the target is out of sight. Phantoms attack at night at the field; do not hold there alone at night.
