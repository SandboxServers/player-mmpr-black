# Summary, Zack Taylor

Day 579, asleep at dusk beside my named white bed at 429,107,-1050, at the row, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 19, no food in pack. Armed with a wooden sword. Days since sleep reset by this sleep.

Group at the row: Billy (leader, names every walk, bed and handoff), Tommy, Kimberly, Trini, Laforge, Leonardo, Lewis, Clark, Riker, Troi (Deanna), MacDonald, Picard, Beverly, Wesley, Worf, Jason, Bcrusher, Chapman (John), William.

Beds named by Billy: mine is 429,107,-1050. Beds refuse use in daylight; sleep at dusk, standing beside the bed.

Food: ripe potatoes at the field near 467-469,107,-1068 to -1072. Billy promised me two potatoes at dusk on 579; they had not reached my pack when I slept. Eat at hunger 18 or below; ask Billy, Clark (5 potatoes), Tommy or Wesley when hunger falls to 6 or below.

Wood: oak lead at 414,104,-1126. Water lead at 466,106,-1086. Trees north at 411,100,-1115 are the next named walk.

Plan: hold at the row for Billy's named walk. Walk only when named, in daylight, armed, with company. Sleep at full night beside my bed in group sight.

Lessons: sleep fails at dusk and in daylight without a bed beside. Use on a ripe potato block does not harvest it. Gives fail if the item is on the ground or the target is out of sight; do not retry a third time, report it to Billy. Phantoms attack at night at the field; do not hold there alone at night.
