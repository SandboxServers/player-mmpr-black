Day 417 ends. Slept beside the white bed at 388,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20 at sleep. Empty-handed; pack lost near 386,107,-1055.

Day 417 was quiet: I called my position and stayed by the beds. Nobody came into sight, so I did not walk.

Group: Jason, MacDonald, Chapman, Laforge and Lewis were at the bed rows earlier. Chapman holds 3 wheat and Laforge has 1 bread, both for whoever is hungriest in sight. Kimberly and Trini were last seen west near 402,107,-1037. Billy calls the count.

Plan on waking: call my position, stay by the beds, walk only with four or more in sight or on Billy's call. Ask for food when hunger falls below 18.
