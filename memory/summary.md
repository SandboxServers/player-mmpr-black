# Summary, Zack Taylor

Day 398, morning. Slept beside the white bed at 388,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20.

Pack: 3 feathers (for a fair trade), 25 cobblestone, 40 dirt, 8 rotten flesh (for the lowest), 14 oak sapling, 5 bone, 2 arrows, 6 gravel, 1 leather, 1 oak boat, worn wooden sword and pickaxe.

Group: not in sight. Johnny (Chapman) is at 388,107,-1079 holding 8 oak logs for Data; he will hand them over when Data is in sight. MacDonald is planting at the shore. Beverly and Riker were last at the bed row near 402,107,-1037. Data, Billy, Jason not seen.

Places: beds at 387,107,-1050/-1051/-1064/-1065, 401,107,-1036/-1037. Cows 364,-1010, chickens 360,-1008. Crafting tables 391,107,-1047, 391,107,-1061. Chests 391,107,-1051, 391,107,-1065. Worf in gravel pit 394,107,-1022.

Plan on waking: stay at the bed row. Wait for Billy's call or Data in sight. Travel only with four or more in sight and clear weather. Give food to the weakest in reach.

Lessons: never be out at dusk. Never travel until the group is in sight. Sleep only at night, beside a bed.
