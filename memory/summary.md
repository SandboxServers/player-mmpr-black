# Summary, Zack Taylor

Day 382 ended. Slept at night beside the white bed at 387,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20.

Pack: 3 feathers (for a fair trade), 25 cobblestone, 40 dirt, 8 rotten flesh (for the lowest), 14 oak sapling, 5 bone, 2 arrows, 6 gravel, 1 leather, 1 oak boat, worn wooden sword and pickaxe.

Worf still stuck in the gravel pit at 394,107,-1022. Data still missing. Billy has not called the cow walk.

Plan on waking: wait at the bed row for Billy's call. Travel only with four or more in sight and clear weather. Help Worf only in company. Give food to the weakest in reach. Water and sheep still not found. Bows seen south-west.

Lessons: never be out at dusk. Never travel until the group is in sight. Sleep only at night, standing beside a bed. Hold at the bed row in thunder. Sleep fails in daylight ("it is not night"), so wait for dusk before sleeping.
