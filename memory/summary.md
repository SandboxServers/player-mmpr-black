# Summary, Zack Taylor

Day 385 ended. Slept at night beside the white bed at 388,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20.

Pack: 3 feathers (for a fair trade), 25 cobblestone, 40 dirt, 8 rotten flesh (for the lowest), 14 oak sapling, 5 bone, 2 arrows, 6 gravel, 1 leather, 1 oak boat, worn wooden sword and pickaxe.

Owed: nothing. Worf owes me 3 melon, not pressing.

Group: scattered near the bed row. Riker at 402,107,-1037 in sight to the west of the row. Chapman has Data's 8 oak logs and will hand them over when Data is in sight. Kimberly was reported low; Trini holds melon. Worf still stuck in the gravel pit at 394,107,-1022. Data still missing. Cow walk not yet called.

Places: beds at 387,107,-1050/-1051/-1064/-1065, 401,107,-1036/-1037, 401,107,-1050/-1051, 401,107,-1064/-1065, 415,107,-1064/-1065, 429,107,-1064/-1065. Cows at 364,-1010, chickens 360,-1008. Crafting tables 391,107,-1047, 391,107,-1061, 405,107,-1047, 405,107,-1033. Chests 391,107,-1051 and 391,107,-1065.

Plan on waking: wait at the bed row for Billy's call. Travel only with four or more in sight and clear weather. Help Worf only in company. Give food to the weakest in reach. Water and sheep still not found. Bows seen south-west.

Lessons: never be out at dusk. Never travel until the group is in sight. Sleep only at night, standing beside a bed. Hold at the bed row in thunder. Sleep fails in daylight, so wait for dusk before sleeping. Intent wake conditions must be valid; wait with interests only.
