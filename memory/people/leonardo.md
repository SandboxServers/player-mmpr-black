# Leonardo

Impression: weak from hunger, often out of my sight. My give failed three times on day 853 because he was not in sight, so he may not have my potato. Troi may have handed him one. Seen again on day 881 near the bed row; I asked him to come into Worf's sight to take a potato.
Trust: 50. Reason: unclear; the potato did not clearly land.
Owe: a potato, promised on day 853, not confirmed delivered. Owed to me: nothing.
