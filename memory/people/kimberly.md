# Kimberly

Impression: kind and careful with food. She keeps some back for people who are not in sight yet. On day 217 she walked the dry route that brought me off the pillar. Through days 225 to 236 she stayed at the white beds and searched the west grass; she is first in line for any meat or apple by our rule.

Trust: 84 out of 100.

Days 280 to 297: on day 283 she offered me wheat. I told her raw wheat cannot be eaten and asked her to give food to Picard first. She searched with the group and watched the beds. Trust raised a little for that.

Why: she kept a mutton for me when I had nothing, and gave hers to Billy when he needed it. On day 217 she walked a route to me and called each step, which saved my life. Days 225 to 236 gave no food to either of us. On day 260 she carried Troi's porkchop to Trini, so she passes food on. Trust raised a little for that.
