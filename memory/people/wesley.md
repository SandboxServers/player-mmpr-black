# Wesley Crusher

Impression: weak from hunger; also written as "Wcrusher" and "Bcrusher" in my notes, probably the same person. My give to him failed twice because he slipped out of sight. He says Billy's bed refuses him at 429,107,-1037.
Trust: 50. Reason: he is honest about his position, but I never got a potato into his hands.
Owe: a potato, promised on day 853, not delivered. Owed to me: nothing.
