# Worf

Impression: took my wool and promised melons, but had not paid by dusk on day 322. Stuck in the gravel pit at 394,107,-1022 with no food on day 365. Since then he has kept the group in sight and asked for potatoes; I gave him one on day 853, and Billy gave him two.
Trust: 50. Reason: the melon trade is still unpaid, but he has held with the group and I received nothing from him for the potatoes.
Owe: nothing now. Owed to me: 3 melons, promised on day 322, unpaid. Do not pay first.
