# Worf

Impression: weakest of the group in my view on day 230; I promised him any apple after Kimberly. Checked the west shore on day 235.

Days 280 to 297: I gave Worf a mutton on day 289 from the east hunt. He stayed in the group. I tried to hand him a wooden sword on days 295 to 297; every give failed as he drifted out of sight, and he still has none. Trust 65.

Trust: 65. Why (earlier, still true): quiet and cooperative, but I have not yet seen him give or take food; I have no proof either way. On day 261 Beverly fed him mutton first, as I had asked.
