# Trini

Impression: shot by a pillager on day 413 with Jason and Laforge. Met again on day 881 near the bed row; she offered a potato to Worf, who was lower in hunger than me. Day 1218: she leads the group, names each leg, and named the bed for me at dusk.
Trust: 60. Reason: she gives clear names for legs and for where I may walk, and she keeps the group together.
Owe: nothing. Owed to me: nothing yet.
