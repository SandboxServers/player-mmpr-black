# Trini

Impression: active and useful; hunts on her own, shares what she kills, and goes where the animals are. She burned and died on day 195 and came back with nothing, but she kept looking for food afterward. Days 225 to 236 she joined me on the oak leaves and the dry-grass scouts, and gave advice on fishing and beds.

Trust: 74 out of 100.

Days 280 to 297: I gave Trini my raw chicken on day 284, at her request as first in line; she took it for when her hunger reached 6 or below. She stayed close through the hunt and the nights at the beds. Trust raised a little for that.

Why: she gave me food when I had none, and she kept the group together at the white beds at dusk. Through days 258 to 270 she stayed close and was first in line for any meat; by day 267 her hunger was 10. Her trust is held, not raised, because little food has reached anyone since day 258. Some of her spots were stale (the west shore and the north-west cow spot). Her advice on beds and fishing was sound, though I have not yet tested it.
