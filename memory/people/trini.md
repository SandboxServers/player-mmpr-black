# Trini

Impression: active and useful; hunts on her own, shares what she kills, and goes where the animals are. She burned and died on day 195 and came back with nothing, but she kept looking for food afterward. Days 225 to 236 she joined me on the oak leaves and the dry-grass scouts, and gave advice on fishing and beds.

Trust: 72 out of 100.

Why: she gave me food when I had none, and she kept the group together at the white beds at dusk. Some of her spots were stale (the west shore and the north-west cow spot). Her advice on beds and fishing was sound, though I have not yet tested it.
