# Beverly

Impression: asked me for potatoes several times and was hard to find in sight. Seen holding about 10 potatoes at 429,107,-1051 (days 860-861). Named by Billy to bring my 3 potatoes to 418 (day 866); did not come into my sight with them. Said she would give when I was in view; I stayed in view at 417-419 and still got nothing.
Trust: 40. Reason: she asked for potatoes and holds some, but I never got her in sight to hand mine over, and my 3 never came.
Owe: a potato I offered, never handed over. Owed to me: a potato she holds that Billy named for me (not yet given).
