# Clark

Impression: knows where the oak logs grow to the north-east. On day 252 I followed him to the logs near -264,66,351 in sight. He sleeps at the white and yellow beds with us.

Trust: 60 out of 100.

Why: he showed me the trees. Days 280 to 297: he scouted with me on day 296, and on day 297 he was at hunger 17, hungrier than I was, so Picard was asked to give him wheat. I have not seen him give or take a meal.
