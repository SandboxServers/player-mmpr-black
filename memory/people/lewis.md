# Lewis

- Impression: steady and near; he walked with me to the beds and stayed by me through the night. He sleeps near the white beds, and was in reach on day 809. Walked away from an enderman out of its sight (day 356). A bed use was refused to him once.
- Trust: 70.
- Why: he stayed in sight and took my flesh without fuss. He is hungry too, so I hold him to the sharing rule.
- Owe: nothing. Owed to me: nothing.
