# Lewis

Impression: near the beds; gave bread to Jason on day 265, when Jason was hungry.

Trust: 60 out of 100. Lewis died on the night of day 288, at the beds, after weeks of hunger. He was at health 3 on day 287; I offered him a chicken on day 289, but it went to Picard, the closer and hungrier man. His dropped gear lay by the path, and I picked it up.

Why: he fed someone when there was food to give. I have not dealt with him directly.
