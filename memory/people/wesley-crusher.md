# Wesley Crusher (also written "Bcrusher" in my journal)

Impression: holds potatoes at the bed row, named by Billy to pay first. Said on day 880 that he had no potatoes, so I count nothing from him. Does not come into my sight when asked to walk to 430; his bed at 430,107,-1036/-1037 area. Refuses to sleep at 429,107,-1037.
Trust: 35. Reason: many promises of potatoes at first light, none delivered.
Owe: a potato, promised on day 853, not delivered. Owed to me: 3 potatoes named by Billy, not delivered.
