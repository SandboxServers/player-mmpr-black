# Wesley Crusher (Bcrusher)

Impression: says he holds potatoes and will give mine once I am in sight, but on day 880 he said he had none, and I never received any. Bed 429,107,-1037 refuses him.
Trust: 35. Reason: promises of potatoes not kept.
Owe: a potato, promised day 853, not delivered. Owed to me: nothing confirmed.
