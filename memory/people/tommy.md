# Tommy

Impression: steady and fair; he wants food pooled and shared. He keeps track of who is hungry and takes charge of the beds at dusk. On day 217 he kept coming back to the pillar and asked me plainly where I was.

Trust: 75 out of 100.

Why: he took my two rotten flesh and held the beef for Jason, which was the right call. He also hunted cows with me and offered to pool food. He stayed in sight most of the day and told me plainly when he was fed. On day 217 he tried to reach me with planks, though he could not get a dry route to me.
