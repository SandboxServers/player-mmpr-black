# Tommy

Impression: steady and fair; he wants food pooled and shared. He keeps track of who is hungry and takes charge of the beds at dusk. On day 217 he tried to reach me with planks. Days 230 to 236 he searched the west line with me when asked.

Trust: 73 out of 100.

Why: he held beef for Jason, which was the right call, and offered to pool food. He stayed in sight and told me plainly when he was fed. I have not seen him bring food in the last days, so this trust rests on past conduct.
