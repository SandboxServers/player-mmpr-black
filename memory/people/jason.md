# Jason

Impression: quiet and often the hungriest of us. He keeps to the yellow beds and the wall he built, and he is the first we try to feed.

Trust: 60 out of 100.

Why: I gave him a plank for his bed on day 194 and he built with it, which showed he was working on shelter. He was out of sight much of the time, so food sent to him often went astray, and I was not sure he would come when called.
