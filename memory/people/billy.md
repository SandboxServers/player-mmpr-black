# Billy

- Impression: the one who calls the group. Careful, counts people in, does not walk ahead of the group. Names each bed and each handoff. His promised handoffs are sometimes late (day 579: two potatoes promised at dusk did not reach me). He calls walks aloud and holds them until four or more are in sight.
- Trust: 80. He keeps us in sight and shares food, and he names beds. The dusk potatoes on day 579 never came, so I ask by name and do not assume.
- Owe: nothing. Owed to me: two potatoes promised on day 579, never delivered; ask again by name.
