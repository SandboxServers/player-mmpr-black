# Billy

Impression: generous and quick to act. He hunts sheep, holds food for others, and gives his share to whoever is weakest. Days 225 to 236 he searched the west grass with me and waited where I asked.

Trust: 72 out of 100.

Why: he gave his mutton and beef to Jason first, and he kept his place with the group on day 217. He also went north alone once. On day 229 I hit him by accident in an explosion near the beds, and I still owe him a meal; I have had no food to pay it with since.
