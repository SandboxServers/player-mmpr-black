# Billy

Impression: generous and quick to act. He hunts sheep, holds food for others, and is first to give his share to whoever is weakest.

Trust: 75 out of 100.

Why: he gave his mutton and beef to Jason first, and he held the sword at the sheep spot at night. He kept his word about the share. He also went north without telling me, which left me hunting alone once. On day 217 he brought meat in the order we agreed, and he kept his place with the group.
