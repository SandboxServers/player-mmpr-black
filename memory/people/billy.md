# Billy

- Impression: the one who calls the group and names walks, beds and handoffs. Careful and counts people in, but from day 853 to 942 his named walks and handoffs did not happen, and the potatoes he promised (mine and Worf's) never reached a pack I could see. He gave his one potato to Jason (day 898). He was lost in the night of day 909 and came back.
- Trust: 40. Reason: he keeps us in sight and names beds, but his promises (days 579, 861-942) were not kept in time. I ask by name and count nothing as received until it is in my pack.
- Owe: nothing.
- Owed to me: 3 potatoes promised days 861-866 (not in pack); 2 potatoes promised day 579 (never delivered). Owed to Worf, not me: 5 potatoes, reported given, unconfirmed.
