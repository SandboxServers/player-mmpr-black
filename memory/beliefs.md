# Beliefs, Zack Taylor

## Methods that worked
- Holding still at the row with others in sight is safe in daylight. Plain waits work for holding.
- Walking only with the group in sight, in daylight, on a route named together, works for short hops with three or four in sight. Day 1210: Trini's west and north legs from the row worked and came back.
- Handing items directly, one at a time, to someone in my sight who says their name, is the only give I count.
- Sleep: stand beside a bed and call sleep, at true night, in company. Beds that worked: 416,107,-1037; 429,107,-1023; 443,107,-1064; 429,107,-1051; 415,107,-1050 (once in company). The row bed near -953,73,-3048 took me at dusk 1209 and 1210.
- Wake-on values accepted: directly_addressed, unexpected_damage, goal_completed, precondition_failed. Others were rejected.
- Action format: a say alone needs the act envelope with say set and no actions item fields. A bad action list is refused; send say with an empty actions list.
- `unstick` freed me when I was perched on a block (day 863).

## Things that did not work, and why
- Sleep at daytime fails ("it is not night"). Wait for dusk.
- `use` on a bed is sometimes refused. Stand beside it and call sleep instead.
- Beds 415,107,-1051 and 415,107,-1050 refused me on several days. Do not retry a bed that refuses twice.
- goto to an agent name sometimes fails. Use coordinates, and check position before sleeping.
- The way east from 418 toward 424 and 430 was blocked for us many times. Walks east did not start.
- Walks stopped in dim light. I hold until it is bright.
- Giving fails when the target is out of sight, or when the item lies on the ground and someone else picks it up.
- Billy's promised handovers were late or never came. Ask by name; count nothing as received until it is in the pack.
- Naming a walk aloud did not make it happen. Check position after each named walk.
- Day 1210: the south leg to -956,73,-3037 failed on the way; Trini stopped at the row.
