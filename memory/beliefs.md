# Beliefs, Zack Taylor

## Methods that worked
- Holding still at the row with Worf and Jason in sight is safe in daylight. Plain waits work for holding.
- Walking only with the group in sight, in daylight, on a route named together, works for short hops with three or four in sight.
- Handing items directly, one at a time, to someone in my sight who says their name, is the only give I count.
- Sleep: stand beside a bed and call sleep, at true night, in company. Beds that worked: 416,107,-1037 (days 754, 762), 429,107,-1023 (days 794, 796), 443,107,-1064 (day 636), 429,107,-1051 (day 809), 430,107,-1051 for Picard (day 707), 415,107,-1050 at night with Jason and Worf in sight (day 877).
- Wake-on values accepted: directly_addressed, unexpected_damage, goal_completed, precondition_failed. Others were rejected.
- To hold a sword in hand: act use with item wooden_sword, aimed at grass or dirt (day 461).
- `unstick` freed me when I was perched on a block (day 863).

## Things that did not work, and why
- Sleep at daytime fails ("it is not night"). Wait for dusk.
- `use` on a bed is sometimes refused ("that block is a bed"). Stand beside it and call sleep instead.
- Bed 415,107,-1051 refused me ("the way was blocked") on days 853-855.
- Bed 415,107,-1050 refused me on day 880 and again on day 935 ("that block is a bed"); Jason slept there on day 935. Do not retry it when it refuses twice.
- Bed 413,107,-1042 was blocked (day 833). Bed 430,107,-1050 blocked once.
- Bed 429,107,-1037 refuses Bcrusher/Wesley. Picard's bed 430,107,-1051 works for Picard.
- goto to an agent name sometimes fails, and a goto can end somewhere other than I aimed. Use coordinates, and check position before sleeping.
- The way east from 418 toward 424 and 430 was blocked for us on days 860-866 about a dozen times, and again in days 904-942. Walks east did not start.
- Walks stopped in dim light. I hold until it is bright.
- Giving fails when the target is out of sight, or when the item lies on the ground and someone else picks it up.
- Gives to MacDonald failed twice when he was out of sight, though he said he was in sight. Position claims are not enough.
- Billy's promised handovers were late or never came: 2 potatoes (day 579), my 3 potatoes (days 861-942, still not in pack), Worf's 5 (reported given days 863 and 866, not seen in Worf's pack). Ask by name; count nothing as received until it is in the pack.
- Bcrusher (Wesley Crusher) said on day 880 that he had no potatoes; I count nothing from him.
- Sword handoffs over distance stall when neither can see the other. Hand within sight only.
- A give can land on the wrong person (day 571). Report it and do not retry a third time.
- Naming a walk aloud did not make it happen. Worf named east walks that were blocked (day 915); the melon walk to 302,80,-822 was asked for on days 936-942 and not yet named or walked.
