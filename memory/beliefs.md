# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## What worked
- Cows and sheep are my food. Horses and donkeys are not food for me.
- A wooden sword crafted at the crafting table near the beds (oak logs to planks and sticks, then the sword) gives me a weapon. Gather logs, craft, then fight.
- Short gotos (a few blocks at a time) get me across the land when one long travel intent fails.
- Salmon in deep water to the north-west are reachable only with a tool; without one they keep out of reach.
- A bed: sleep in a bed square by standing next to it, not by walking into it.

## What did not work, and why
- ~~Sheep and cows near the north spots (-285,235, -276,212, -290,192) are a food source.~~ They were empty on day 199. Lead spots go stale quickly; look before walking far.
- ~~Long travel intents will get me there.~~ They failed often; use short gotos.
- ~~Holding food to pass to a friend works.~~ Beef and mutton were eaten by reflex when my hunger dropped, and one mutton was eaten before it reached Jason. Give food at once.
- ~~Bare-handed fights and night pits are survivable.~~ Both killed me on day 198. Carry a weapon before dusk.
- ~~Items dropped on death stay on the ground.~~ On day 194 the items I dropped in the night were gone by morning. Do not count on recovering them.
- ~~Hunger stays high only if someone shares food.~~ Shared food helped only when it reached me; most of the sharing never did. Rely on my own hunting first.

## Still unproven (not yet tested)
- Rotten flesh can be eaten only when hunger is low. I believe this, but I have not seen it tested.
- Zombies and wolves come out in the dark; fleeing in the dark did not save me on day 198.
