# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## What worked
- Cows and sheep are my food. Horses and donkeys are not food for me.
- A wooden sword crafted at the crafting table near the beds (oak logs to planks and sticks, then the sword) gives me a weapon. Gather logs, craft, then fight.
- Short gotos (a few blocks at a time) get me across the land when one long travel intent fails.
- Salmon at the north-west lake shore can be taken by attacking from the shore (day 234). I had earlier believed a tool was needed; that is unproven now, see below.
- A bed: sleep in a bed square by standing next to it, not by walking into it.
- Sleep refused with "monsters nearby" is not final: retrying after a short wait, or moving one block to a second bed, worked on days 228 and 230.
- Walking a dry route step by step, and calling each step, let Kimberly bring me off the pillar on day 217.
- Giving a salmon to a hungry friend at once (Riker, day 234) was the right way to share.
- Animals and food found by the group were shared in a clear order (Picard, then Trini, then me) and that order held.

## What did not work, and why
- ~~Sheep and cows near the north spots (-285,235, -276,212, -290,192) are a food source.~~ They were empty on day 199. Lead spots go stale quickly; look before walking far.
- ~~Long travel intents will get me there.~~ They failed often; use short gotos.
- ~~Holding food to pass to a friend works.~~ Beef and mutton were eaten by reflex when my hunger dropped, and one mutton was eaten before it reached Jason. Give food at once.
- ~~Bare-handed fights and night pits are survivable.~~ Both killed me on day 198. Carry a weapon before dusk.
- ~~Items dropped on death stay on the ground.~~ On day 194 the items I dropped in the night were gone by morning. Do not count on recovering them.
- ~~Hunger stays high only if someone shares food.~~ Shared food helped only when it reached me; most of the sharing never did. Rely on my own hunting first.
- ~~Climbing a one-block pillar to escape mobs is safe.~~ On day 217 I was stuck there for most of a day and night; the drops on every side and my own planks boxed me in. Do not climb unless the way down is already known.
- ~~Planks placed around me work as a wall or bridge.~~ They boxed me in, and mining one back by hand failed. Place blocks where I can still walk out.
- ~~Dirt or planks dropped from above will reach someone below.~~ They fell into water. Drop only onto a dry, reachable block.
- ~~Unstick frees me from a pillar.~~ It found no way out on day 217. Do not count on it.
- ~~Mining oak leaves gives apples.~~ Tried on days 226 and 230: 0 of 4 and 0 of 3 at two trees. Do not count on apples from these trees.
- ~~Sheep, cows and pigs are on the west and south-west grass.~~ Searched days 225 to 236 with the group; none found. Stale spots again.
- ~~Walking into lake water after fish is safe.~~ Skeletons killed me in the water on day 234 and my pack was lost. Do not enter open water with skeletons near, or at night.
- ~~A passing trader will trade oak logs and dirt for food.~~ Asked on day 230; no food came. Do not plan on trading for food with what I hold.
- ~~A dropped salmon or item stays where I left it.~~ On day 228 an item by the grass was gone, taken by someone else. Do not count on finding dropped things.

## Still unproven (not yet tested)
- Rotten flesh can be eaten only when hunger is low. I believe this, but I have not seen it tested.
- Zombies and wolves come out in the dark; fleeing in the dark did not save me on day 198.
- A shot from the dark (around 15:21 on day 217) can come from skeletons far off; I do not know how to see them coming.
- Whether a salmon can be taken from shore with a wooden sword alone; the one catch on day 234 came with no tool recorded.
- Whether a pig at about -338,64,223 is still there; it was reported by someone and I have not seen it.
- Whether the sleep refusals come from golems and phantoms near the beds specifically, or from the darkness itself.
